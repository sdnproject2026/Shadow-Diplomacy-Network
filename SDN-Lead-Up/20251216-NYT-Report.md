# New York Times Epstein Report

The New York Times published several reports about Jeffrey Epstein on Thursday, December 18, 2025, focusing on his connections with President Trump and the latest revelations from ongoing investigations.

### December 18, 2025

The New York Times released a detailed report titled “Don’s Best Friend: How Epstein and Trump Bonded Over the Pursuit of Women,” examining the nature and extent of their relationship, including how their shared interest in women shaped their interactions and public personas.
- [1]

### December 16, 2025

The Times published a major investigative piece titled “Scams, Schemes, Ruthless Cons: The Untold Story of How Jeffrey Epstein Got Rich,” revealing the origins of Epstein’s wealth, including a series of scams and cons that allowed him to amass a fortune despite lacking formal financial training.
- [2][8]

A follow-up article summarized six key takeaways from the investigation, clarifying that Epstein’s wealth came from a mix of luck, deception, and manipulation of sophisticated investors.
- [3]

### November 12, 2025

The Times reported on newly uncovered emails where Epstein alleged that President Trump spent hours at his house with one of Epstein’s victims, raising questions about Trump’s awareness of Epstein’s criminal conduct.
- [6]

### July 15, 2025

The Department of Justice announced that its investigation found no evidence Epstein maintained a “client list,” blackmailed powerful figures, or was murdered, reaffirming the official finding that Epstein died by suicide in 2019.
- [10]

### July 7, 2025

The DOJ released a memo stating that no credible evidence was found to support claims that Epstein blackmailed prominent individuals, leading to widespread skepticism from political commentators.
- [4]
