# Staff Performing "House Calls"

Recent investigative reports and court filings released in early January 2026 have shed new light on the historical connection between President Donald Trump’s Mar-a-Lago club and Jeffrey Epstein. These disclosures, stemming from the ongoing release of the "Epstein Files," detail the practice of club staff performing "house calls" at Epstein’s nearby mansion.

## December 19, 2025

The original federal deadline for the Department of Justice to release all files related to Jeffrey Epstein passed, with the department currently in violation of the law as only a small fraction of documents have been published.

## December 23, 2025

The DOJ released a batch of 30,000 pages containing FBI files, some of which included previously unverified allegations of misconduct involving Trump and Epstein, which the White House dismissed as "unfounded and false."

## December 31, 2025

A report detailed how Mar-a-Lago’s spa staff, including several teenage workers, were allegedly sent to Epstein’s Palm Beach mansion to provide massages and manicures in the late 1990s and early 2000s.

## January 5, 2026

Legal filings revealed that the Department of Justice has reviewed less than 1% of the total 2 million documents responsive to the Epstein Files Transparency Act, leading to accusations of an administration "cover-up."

## January 6, 2026

New scrutiny focused on a 2003 incident where an 18-year-old Mar-a-Lago spa worker allegedly reported being pressured for sex by Epstein during a house call, leading to Trump’s decision to ban Epstein from the club.
