# Compiling Epstein's Emails

- On November 12, 2025, the US House Oversight Committee announced a massive document dump from Jeffrey Epstein's estate, consisting of about 20,000 files including thousands of emails covering topics such as women, blackmail, and holiday gatherings with Donald Trump. COURIER created an easily searchable database from these documents using Google Pinpoint to improve access for the public. Donald Trump's name appears more than any other in these emails.
- [5]

- By November 13, 2025, reports indicated that Donald Trump's name appears in 1,628 of the 20,000 released documents, more than anyone else. This searchable database enables people to explore the files in detail.
- [2][4]

- On November 18, 2025, social media posts highlighted that COURIER compiled 23,000 Epstein emails into a searchable database, emphasizing that Donald Trump's name appears more than any other, just within these emails. It is suggested that the total files amount to 100,000, implying many more mentions might exist beyond the current dataset.
- [1][3]

- The US House Oversight Committee's document dump and COURIER's searchable database have brought attention to the frequency of Trump's mentions in the emails, but some discussions note that many references predate his public political career. There are ongoing efforts to release more documents blocked previously.
- [4][5]
