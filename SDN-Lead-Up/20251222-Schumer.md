# Chuck Schumer to sue DOJ

Senate Democrats, led by Minority Leader Chuck Schumer, are moving to sue the Trump Department of Justice (DOJ) over the handling of the Epstein files, accusing the administration of a cover-up due to the incomplete and heavily redacted release of documents.

## December 19, 2025

Deadline for DOJ to release Epstein files as required by the Epstein Files Transparency Act.

DOJ released only a fraction of the files, with massive redactions, including entire documents blacked out.

Senate Democrats, including Schumer, accused the Trump administration of violating the law and engaging in a cover-up.
- [1][9][12][13]

## December 20, 2025

Schumer announced plans to introduce a Senate resolution that would authorize the Senate to file or join lawsuits against the DOJ to compel full co9mpliance with the transparency law.
- [3][10]

## December 21, 2025

Schumer formally urged Senate Democrats to take legal action, criticizing the DOJ’s incremental and redacted release of records as a “blatant cover-up” and a violation of both the spirit and letter of the law.
- [10]

## December 22, 2025

Schumer plans to force a vote on the resolution when the Senate reconvenes after the holiday recess, putting pressure on Senate Republicans to either support legal action or face political consequences for inaction.
- [1][3]
