# Pam Bondi In Contempt

Ro Khanna and Thomas Massie are seeking to hold Attorney General Pam Bondi in contempt and impose daily fines for not releasing the full Epstein-related files.

## December 20, 2025

Khanna and Massie warn they may pursue inherent contempt against AG Pam Bondi over incomplete Epstein files release. They describe potential daily fines as a lever to compel full disclosure. This reflects their Epstein Files Transparency Act effort and ongoing frustration with redactions and partial releases.
- [5][7]

## December 21, 2025

Public reporting indicates Khanna and Massie are pushing for Bondi to be held in contempt as the DOJ’s document dump arrives, which critics say still falls short of full, non-redacted disclosure. The dispute is framed as part of a broader battle over transparency and victims’ rights.
- [1][2]

## December 22, 2025

Coverage notes continued discussion of contempt or impeachment possibilities as the Epstein file release remains contested, with lawmakers arguing that the DOJ has not met statutory or ethical obligations for full disclosure.
- [3]

### Note: 

Several outlets report similar developments, including references to inherent contempt proposals and potential impeachment discussions tied to the Epstein files timeline.
- [4][9]
