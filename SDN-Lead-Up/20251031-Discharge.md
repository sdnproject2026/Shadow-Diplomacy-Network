## Specific Resolution

The specific resolution that the discharge petition led by Congressman Thomas Massie was related to is a motion to discharge a committee from the consideration of a resolution concerning the release of files related to Jeffrey Epstein. This motion was officially filed on September 2, 2025, to force a House floor vote on making those Epstein-related documents public.

The resolution itself is a procedural step to bypass committee or leadership blockage and bring the matter directly to the House for a vote.

## Key points

- The discharge petition targets a resolution to release Epstein files.
- Filed on September 2, 2025.
- Aims to force a vote despite opposition from House leadership.

# Status

Outcome of the Discharge Petition Led by Thomas Massie on Jeffrey Epstein Files

As of late September 2025, Congressman Thomas Massie announced he had secured the 218 signatures required to force a House floor vote on the release of Jeffrey Epstein-related files.

Despite reaching the signature threshold, the vote was delayed because House Speaker Mike Johnson kept the House in recess, which Massie publicly criticized as an obstruction to transparency.

The petition created significant pressure on House leadership but did not immediately result in a floor vote due to procedural delays and political resistance.

GOP support for the petition showed signs of erosion in early September, indicating some wavering among Republicans despite initial bipartisan momentum.

As of early November 2025, the petition remains a contentious issue, with Massie continuing to push for the vote and accusing leadership of blocking progress.

## Summary

The discharge petition successfully gathered enough signatures to force a vote but was stalled by House procedural maneuvers and leadership opposition, delaying the release of Epstein files.
