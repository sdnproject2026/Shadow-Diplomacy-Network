# Susie Wiles formal inquiry

The news regarding White House Chief of Staff Susie Wiles involves a formal inquiry from the Senate Judiciary Committee following her public admissions about sensitive Jeffrey Epstein documents.

In a mid-December 2025 profile with Vanity Fair, Wiles stated that she had read the "Epstein file" and confirmed that President Trump's name appears on the flight manifests, though she maintained he did nothing "awful." This admission sparked immediate legal and ethical concerns regarding how a high-level political advisor gained access to potentially sealed or grand jury-protected documents before their official public release.

### November 19, 2025i

President Trump signs the bipartisan Epstein Files Transparency Act into law, requiring the Department of Justice to begin the process of reviewing and releasing over one million pages of investigative records.

### December 16, 2025

-Vanity Fair publishes a two-part profile on Susie Wiles. In it, she claims to have reviewed "the Epstein file," specifically noting that Trump and Epstein were "young, single playboys together" and that Trump is "on the manifest" of Epstein's plane.

### December 22, 2025

Senators Sheldon Whitehouse (D-RI) and Dick Durbin (D-IL) send a formal letter to Wiles demanding transparency regarding her access to these materials.

### December 30, 2025

The Senate letter is made public. It lists specific demands for Wiles to explain the authority under which she accessed the files, whether the materials were grand jury secrets, and if she shared the contents with the President.

### January 5, 2026

The official deadline set by the Senators for Wiles to provide a written response to the inquiry.
