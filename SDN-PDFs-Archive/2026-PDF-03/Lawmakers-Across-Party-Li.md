Back Epstein Special Prosecutor
Frustration grows with Pam Bondi’s lack of arrests and handling of the

Instagram

Bipartisan support is building for appointing a special prosecutor to oversee the release
of the Epstein files. Republican Rep. Nancy Mace said she has consistently backed a
special prosecutor and full disclosure, adding, “I think the biggest question is why didn’t
more people go to jail?”
Rep. Tim Burchett expressed frustration with Attorney General Pam Bondi, saying, “I
think it’s time to just have a prosecutor that will prosecute some people in this town.”
Rep. Becca Balint echoed the call for an Epstein special prosecutor saying, “100 percent.
Yeah.”
