alleged abuse around 1983–85.

secure review facility this week, with Rep. Robert Garcia saying a survivor made
serious allegations about the president, and a DOJ spokesperson stating
documents may be temporarily removed for redactions and nothing was improperly
withheld.
Buried in more than 3 million pages released by the DOJ are materials prosecutors
