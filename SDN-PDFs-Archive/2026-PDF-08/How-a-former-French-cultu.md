Epstein's Parisian

Former culture minister and former president of the Arab World Institute Jack Lang is under
investigation by France's National Financial Prosecutor's Office over his links to convicted sex
offender Jeffrey Epstein, who died in prison in 2019. Lang's daughter, Caroline Lang, is also under
investigation. They are suspected of "aggravated tax fraud laundering," particularly in connection
with an offshore company based in the Virgin Islands that Epstein set up in 2016 in association with
Caroline Lang, and the sale of a riad in Morocco.
