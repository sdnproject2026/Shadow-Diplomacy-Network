Jeffrey Epstein, a convicted sex offender whose death by suicide has
spawned intense scrutiny of the high-profile people he knew, mentioned
Donald Trump by name multiple times in private correspondence over
the last 15 years with an associate and an author in Trump’s orbit,
according to newly released emails from Democrats on the House
Oversight Committee.
