JD Vance’s Old Epstein Tweets
Come Back To Bite Him

Everything old was new again for Vice President JD Vance on Wednesday
after social media posts he made about the Jeffrey Epstein scandal from
2021 resurfaced.
The timing was awkward for the vice president considering Democrats on
the House Oversight Committee released three shocking emails from
Epstein where he hints to confidants just how aware Donald Trump was of
his criminal acts.
After the emails became public, CNN’s Andrew Kaczynski reposted two
tweets Vance made in 2021 that discuss the Epstein scandal.
In one of the posts, Vance says he thinks about a 2019 column from “The
Week” headlined, “The Jeffrey Epstein case is why people believe in
Pizzagate,” “about once a month.”
The article focuses on whether Trump was “speaking from personal
experience when he said in 2002 that Epstein ‘likes beautiful women as
much as I do, and many of them are on the younger side.’”
I think about this @matthewwalther column about once a month:

In a follow-up tweet, Vance noted that the scandal at the time seemed to
have been swept under the carpet and wondered why that might be.
“Remember when we learned that our wealthiest and most powerful
people were connected to a guy who ran a literal child sex trafficking
ring?” Vance wrote on his account. “And then that guy died mysteriously in
a jail? And now we just don’t talk about it.”
