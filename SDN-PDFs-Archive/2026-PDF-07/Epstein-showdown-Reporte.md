Reporter Katie Phang recently nabbed a colossal win against
interim AG Todd Blanche and his crusade to keep the Epstein
files under wraps.

After months of stalling by the administration of President

Donald Trump and ignoring the letter of a new law demanding
the release of the sex-trafficker’s files, U.S. District Judge Emmet
G. Sullivan opened the floodgates on Trump’s longtime
friendship. Sullivan sided with former MS NOW show host Katie
Phang in her lawsuit demanding the Trump administration
adhere to the Epstein Transparency Act.
Phang told Left Hook founder Wajahat Ali that this means
Blanche himself must stand before the judge on Thursday and
either deliver the redacted information or be prepared to
explain why he won’t.
This, said Phang, puts Blanche and the president that he protects
in an ugly predicament.
“It is an interesting trap that has been laid by Judge Sullivan for
Todd Blanche, which is why we have been successful each and
every step of the way, and that's the reason why we really do
have him against the ropes," said Phang. "So let's see what he
does, because right now he himself, Todd Blanche individually,
now has to go and deal with a judge on this.”
Trump, she said, is a historic staller when it comes to litigation
he doesn't like, but running down the clock no longer appears to
be an option for the frustrated president, who was a longtime
friend of the sex-trafficker before his death.

which is the federal appeals court above Judge Sullivan, and he'd
have to get an immediate emergency stay.”
