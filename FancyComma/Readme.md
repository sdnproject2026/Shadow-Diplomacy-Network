# Fancy Comma, LLC

- https://fancycomma.com

- February 2020

Science communications and policy consulting firm founded by neuroscientist-journalist Sheeva Azma, specializing in translating complex science, health, technology, business, finance, and policy topics into strategic communications, technical content, journalism, and policy analysis

The firm specializes in communicating complex subjects across science, health, technology, finance, business, and policy, with services spanning scientific and technical writing, white papers, policy research and analysis, strategic communications, digital marketing, public relations, executive communications, speechwriting, and journalism.
