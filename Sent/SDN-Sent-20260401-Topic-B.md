### Do Not Constitute Official Duties

By determining that actions taken in the capacity of an office-seeker or candidate do not constitute official duties, the court has opened a pathway for civil litigation and broad discovery into activities previously shielded by the outer perimeter of official responsibility.

### Epstein Files Coverup

In the context of the alleged Epstein files coverup, this ruling suggests that officials who engaged in obstructive acts or money laundering under the guise of official state business may be stripped of immunity if those actions are deemed personal, political, or part of a racketeering enterprise.

### Pardons Do Not Offer A Shield

The ruling clarifies that federal pardons do not offer a shield against state-level RICO charges or civil conspiracy claims.

### State Prosecutors

For the Epstein case, this means that state prosecutors in jurisdictions like New York or Florida, and international bodies investigating money laundering, can leverage the distinction between official acts and private conspiracies to pierce through immunity claims and access previously protected communications and financial records.
