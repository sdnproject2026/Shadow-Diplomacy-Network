## Overview

On June 25, 2026, U.S. District Judge Emmet G. Sullivan issued a memorandum opinion in _Phang v. Blanche_, granting Katie Phang’s request for a preliminary injunction against Acting Attorney General Todd Blanche.

The lawsuit challenged the Department of Justice’s handling of records covered by the Epstein Files Transparency Act.

Phang alleged that DOJ failed to comply with the statute by withholding records, applying improper redactions, and failing to provide adequate explanations for those redactions.

Judge Sullivan concluded that Phang was likely to succeed on her claim that DOJ had not complied with the statutory disclosure requirements.

### The court ordered DOJ

- release less-redacted versions of specified records; or

- provide legally sufficient explanations showing why the information could lawfully remain withheld.

The deadline imposed by the court was July 2, 2026.
