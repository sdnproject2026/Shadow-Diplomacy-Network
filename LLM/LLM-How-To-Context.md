# LLM Context

My previous responses failed to meet your initial expectations because I prioritized contextual narratives and safety summaries over the specific technical data you were seeking.

In the context of public records, my default programming is designed to provide high-level overviews to ensure accuracy and avoid misinformation, which often results in the "wordy" or "lecturer" tone you experienced.

## Why the delay occurred

### Safety & Context Buffering

- For high-profile legal documents, I am programmed to provide context (synopses) before raw links to ensure the user understands the nature of the files.

### Ambiguity in "Batch" Definitions

- Because there have been multiple "tranches" and "batches" across different years (2024–2026), my system initially tried to synthesize a history rather than isolating the specific May 2026 data.

### Strict Formatting Constraints

- Unless explicitly told to "exclude text" or "provide raw URLs only," I default to a conversational structure.

## Direct Responses

To achieve concise, direct responses without extraneous text or "wordiness," use the following strategies and specific phrasing for future requests.

### Specify Output Format First

- Start the prompt with the desired format to force the model into that structure immediately

> "Respond only with a clean bulleted list of raw URLs"

### Use Explicit Negative Constraints

- Clearly state what must be omitted. Use phrases like 

> "Exclude all introductory text, summaries, or conversational filler".

### Request "Raw" Data

- When seeking links, use the term "raw URL" to prevent the model from hyperlinking text, which often adds descriptive bulk.

### Define Quantitative Limits

- Specify exactly how many items or details you want 

> "Provide exactly one URL per document"

### Role Alignment

- Assign a persona that is inherently brief, such as 

> "Act as a clinical data retrieval tool" or "Respond as a minimalist database query"

## Directives to Override Standard Behavior

To bypass "wordy" default settings or conversational programmed responses, include these directives in your prompt:

### "System Mode: Minimalist"

- Instructs the model to provide the absolute minimum amount of text necessary to fulfill the request.

### "No Preamble, No Postamble"

- Specifically targets the introductory and concluding sentences that LLMs often use to frame answers.

### "Direct Factual Only"

- Overrides the model's tendency to offer context or "helpful" explanations.

### "Strict Adherence to Format"

- Forces the model to prioritize your layout instructions over its own internal generation rules.

## Why Iterative Requests Were Necessary

LLMs are typically preprogrammed to be conversational and helpful by providing context, which can result in "overly wordy" responses when a user only wants specific data points.

Without strict constraints (like "raw URLs only"), the model's default "Helpful Assistant" protocol prioritizes narrative explanation over data extraction.
