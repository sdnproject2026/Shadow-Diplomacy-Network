# Small-project CAD AI

## PrintPal CAD Agent

-  browser-based OpenSCAD editor with an AI design assistant for quick parametric parts and small maker projects.

- https://www.printpal.io/cad-agent

## Leo AI

-  text-to-CAD focused on mechanical design workflows and quick concept generation.

- https://www.getleo.ai/blog/best-free-text-to-cad-tools

## AutoCAD AI

-  listed as a free, no-signup AI tool for generating architectural/CAD-style outputs.

- https://theresanaiforthat.com/@wilsonabella/autocad-ai/

## AI for That

-  a directory of CAD-related AI tools, useful for finding more free-tier options.

## ChatCAD
  - Website: https://chatcad.ai
  - Free tier: 50 AI-generated models/month
  - Credit card required: No
  - Exports: STEP, STL, engineering drawings
  - Best for: Mechanical parts and assemblies

## TextoCAD
  - Website: https://textocad.com
  - Free tier: Free to use
  - Credit card required: No
  - Exports: STEP, STL
  - Best for: Parametric text-to-CAD with editable models

## Prompt2CAD
  - Website: https://prompt2cad.com
  - Free tier: 120 free credits
  - Credit card required: No
  - Exports: STL, OBJ (STEP/DXF available after top-up)
  - Best for: Fast concept modeling

## BuildCAD AI
  - Website: https://buildcad.ai
  - Free tier: Free plan available
  - Credit card required: No
  - Exports: STEP, IGES, STL
  - Best for: Browser-based production CAD and collaboration

## PartWork AI
  - Website: https://www.partwork.ai
  - Free tier: Free to start
  - Credit card required: No
  - Best for: AI-generated functional parts, CAD viewing, and conversion
