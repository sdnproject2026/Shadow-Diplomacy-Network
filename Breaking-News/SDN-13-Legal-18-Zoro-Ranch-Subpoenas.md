# Zoro Ranch 14 subpoenas

On June 1, 2026, the New Mexico Survivor's Truth Commission held its inaugural public meeting and officially announced the issuance of 14 subpoenas. 

## Key Details of the Investigation

- The Commission: This is a bipartisan panel of four state lawmakers—two Democrats and two Republicans—established by the New Mexico House of Representatives. It is chaired by Democratic Representative Andrea Romero. 

- The Target: The panel is probing Jeffrey Epstein’s notorious Zorro Ranch, a sprawling property located south of Santa Fe, New Mexico. 

- Entities Subpoenaed: The 14 initial subpoenas target institutional files rather than individuals. They demand records from federal and local agencies, including the U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ), the FBI, the New Mexico Department of Justice, the Santa Fe County Sheriff’s Office, Epstein's estate, major financial institutions like Deutsche Bank, and the Santa Fe Institute. 

- The Objectives: Lawmakers aim to uncover why the federal government failed to aggressively investigate the ranch for decades and determine if local systems or enablers covered up or ignored Epstein's sex trafficking enterprise.

As of mid-June 2026, the commission has begun publishing the physical copies of these subpoenas online, giving the targeted agencies until June 30, 2026, to comply.
