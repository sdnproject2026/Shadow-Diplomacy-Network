

# Melanua - Ungaro - Zampolli

A reported interview with Amanda Ungaro describing her deportation from the United States, her past relationship with Paolo Zampolli, and her brief encounter with Jeffrey Epstein's circle.

Commentary and speculation from third parties connecting Ungaro's allegations to Melania Trump, Donald Trump, Epstein-related narratives, and broader political claims.

A news report discussing allegations that Paolo Zampolli contacted senior immigration officials after Ungaro's arrest.

The core verified reporting centers on Amanda Ungaro, a Brazilian former model who says she was deported from the United States in 2025 after spending years living there.

According to her account, she was arrested alongside her husband in connection with alleged fraud at a cosmetic clinic, denied wrongdoing, and was subsequently transferred into ICE custody.

She spent several months in immigration detention before being removed to Brazil.

A major focus of the interview is her long-running conflict with Paolo Zampolli, a businessman, former modeling executive, and Trump associate who is also the father of her son.

Ungaro alleges that Zampolli used his political connections to influence immigration authorities and strengthen his position in an ongoing custody dispute.

She also alleges years of psychological, sexual, and physical abuse.

Zampolli denies those allegations and disputes claims that he improperly influenced her detention or deportation.

The interview also revisits Ungaro's modeling career.

She claims that as a teenager she flew on Jeffrey Epstein's private aircraft after being invited by modeling agent Jean-Luc Brunel.

She describes seeing Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell during the flight but says she had no meaningful interaction with them afterward and never saw Epstein again.

Another section covers Ungaro's diplomatic work.

After leaving modeling, she served in a diplomatic capacity connected to Grenada at the United Nations while Zampolli represented Dominica.

She says that for years her immigration status remained unresolved and that she was in the process of obtaining a different visa when she was arrested.

The accompanying commentary dramatically expands beyond the reported facts.

It speculates that Ungaro may possess information regarding the origins of Donald Trump's relationship with Melania Trump and that she could potentially reveal damaging information about members of the Trump circle.

It references social media posts allegedly made by Ungaro that contain threats to expose corruption and pursue legal action.

However, the commentary provides claims and interpretations that are not independently verified within the supplied material.

Assertions regarding what Ungaro may know, what she may reveal, whether specific allegations about Melania Trump are true, or whether any individuals committed criminal acts are presented as speculation or opinion rather than established fact.

The Independent article largely focuses on reporting that Zampolli allegedly contacted senior ICE officials after learning of Ungaro's arrest.

According to that reporting, Zampolli denies seeking favors or special treatment.

Homeland Security officials similarly deny that Ungaro's detention and removal were politically motivated.

## Five Key Points

1. Amanda Ungaro says she was deported from the United States after spending months in immigration detention following her arrest in a cosmetic clinic fraud case that she denies

2. The central conflict involves a bitter custody dispute between Ungaro and her former partner Paolo Zampolli, who denies her allegations of abuse and denies improperly influencing immigration authorities

3. Reporting from major outlets cited in the articles states that Zampolli contacted senior immigration officials after learning of Ungaro's arrest, though officials deny political favoritism and Zampolli denies wrongdoing

4. Ungaro claims she flew on Jeffrey Epstein's private plane as a teenager through modeling agent Jean-Luc Brunel, but says she had only a brief interaction with Epstein and did not maintain any relationship with him

5. Commentary attached to the articles speculates that Ungaro may possess information concerning Donald Trump, Melania Trump, Epstein-related connections, and events from the modeling world, but those claims remain unverified within the provided material.
