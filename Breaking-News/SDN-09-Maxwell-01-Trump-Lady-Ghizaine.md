## Robert Maxwell 

###  Yacht _Lady Ghislaine_ 

Published Record, Guests, Parties, Meetings, and Logs

### Scope

- I checked the supplied article against contemporary reporting, the 2025 US Department of Justice transcript, and other published sources.

- I found substantial reporting about parties, guests, business activity, and the yacht's final voyage, but I did not find a publicly authenticated, complete set of the vessel's ship logs.

- Where a detail comes from later recollection, a secondary source, or an allegation, it is identified as such rather than presented as established fact.

Robert Maxwell was the British publishing magnate and politician who owned the yacht _Lady Ghislaine_.

### Emad Khashoggi

The vessel had been built in the Netherlands and was acquired by Maxwell in 1986 after originally being commissioned for businessman Emad Khashoggi.

Maxwell renamed it after his youngest daughter, Ghislaine.

Published descriptions put the yacht at approximately 180 feet long.

Contemporary reporting described it as having a gymnasium and discotheque; other accounts describe a jacuzzi and sauna as well.

One later account says it could accommodate approximately 12 guests and 13 crew.

### Ghislaine Maxwell

Ghislaine Maxwell spent substantial time aboard the yacht during the late 1980s, and photographs from the period show Robert Maxwell, Ghislaine Maxwell and Elisabeth Maxwell aboard it.

### Robert Maxwell's Death

The yacht subsequently became famous as the vessel from which Robert Maxwell disappeared in November 1991.

## The 1989 New York Yacht Party

The most important published record concerning the yacht and Donald Trump is the May 1989 New York party.

Contemporary society reporting described Donald Trump attending a party aboard Robert Maxwell's yacht.

Ghislaine Maxwell was also present.

The reported guest list included:

- Robert Maxwell

- Ghislaine Maxwell

- Donald Trump

- John Tower

- John Lehman

- Thomas Pickering

- Tom Bolan

- Morton Janklow

- Peter Kalikow

- Helene Atkin

Some later accounts also identify 

- Steve Ross 

- Mike Wallace 

among people photographed or reported as attending. 

Because different reproductions of the original society report give somewhat different guest lists, those additional names should be treated separately from the core contemporary list.

### Donald Trump and David Adler

The Washington Post later obtained a recollection from David Adler, then a public-relations executive working for Maxwell's publishing operation.

According to Adler, Maxwell greeted Trump privately in the yacht's wheelhouse and showed him around the vessel.

Adler described the interaction as business-oriented, saying the two men were effectively trying to get business from each other.

He also said he pitched Trump on the idea of Robert Maxwell publishing a Trump-branded magazine.

Trump reportedly expressed interest, but the proposal did not proceed.

Contemporary reporting also described Trump comparing yachts with Maxwell.

### Ivana Trump

Trump attended without Ivana Trump.

### The yacht comparison

Trump already owned the _Trump Princess_.

Published accounts note that Trump was interested in the size and features of Maxwell's vessel and that the two men compared their yachts.

The two vessels also had an unusual connection: _Lady Ghislaine_ had previously been associated with the Khashoggi family, while Trump's _Trump Princess_ had been purchased from Adnan Khashoggi.

### Caviar and Concorde

The 1989 society coverage is also notable for its description of luxury provisions at the party.

Later reproductions of the contemporary reporting describe caviar being flown in from Paris aboard the Concorde.

This detail is frequently repeated in later accounts of the event, but it should not be confused with evidence of a separate Maxwell business meeting or intelligence operation.

The published evidence establishes it as part of the party's extravagant catering.

### Shoes

One published account recalls that guests were required to remove their shoes before boarding.

Trump reportedly complied, despite being reluctant to remove his shoes, and wore protective blue footwear aboard the yacht.

### Maxwell's interest in the guest list

Later accounts say Robert Maxwell personally took an interest in the guest list.

The published list included a mixture of political, diplomatic, publishing, legal, media and business figures.

That mixture is significant historically because the event was not simply a private family gathering: 

- it brought together people from several of Maxwell's commercial and political circles.

## 1990 Yatch Party

### Another Documented Party

There is also photographic evidence of a 1990 party aboard _Lady Ghislaine_.

Photographs reproduced by Town & Country identify Robert Maxwell, Elisabeth Maxwell and Ghislaine Maxwell aboard the yacht.

The existence of this 1990 party is therefore independent of the 1989 Trump event.

The published sources do not provide a complete authenticated guest manifest for the 1990 party.

### Ghislaine Relationship With The Yacht

Ghislaine Maxwell was not merely the yacht's namesake.

Published biographical accounts say she spent considerable time aboard _Lady Ghislaine_ during the 1980s.

The vessel functioned as part of the social world surrounding the Maxwell family.

Accounts describe it as a party and entertaining venue equipped with luxury amenities including:

- jacuzzi

- sauna

- gym

- discotheque

- large guest accommodations

- professional crew

The yacht therefore served both as a luxury residence/entertainment platform and as a setting for Maxwell's business and social contacts.

### Business conducted aboard _Lady Ghislaine_

The yacht was not exclusively used for parties.

Contemporary reporting states that Robert Maxwell often conducted business aboard the vessel.

This becomes particularly important in 1991, when the yacht was used as a base while Maxwell was dealing with the acquisition of the New York Daily News and other business matters.

A later Guardian account by journalist Mark Tran describes interviewing Maxwell aboard _Lady Ghislaine_ in New York in 1991.

### 1991 The Final Voyage

The most extensively documented voyage of _Lady Ghislaine_ was the journey that ended with Robert Maxwell's death.

### 1991-10-31

- Robert Maxwell traveled from Britain toward Gibraltar.

- He first flew by helicopter to Luton.

- He then boarded his Gulfstream jet.

- He reached Gibraltar at approximately 10:50 a.m.

- _Lady Ghislaine_ was waiting there.

### 1991-10-31 - 1991-11-04

- The yacht proceeded from Gibraltar toward Madeira.

- Its first reported port of call was Funchal, Madeira.

- It subsequently traveled to Santa Cruz de Tenerife in the Canary Islands.

### 1991-11-04

- _Lady Ghislaine_ docked at Santa Cruz de Tenerife.

- Maxwell went ashore that evening.

- Reports say he became angry with the yacht's crew because they had not obtained lobster.

- He took a taxi into Santa Cruz.

- He eventually ate at the Hotel Mencey.

- He ate codfish with clams and mushrooms and drank approximately three beers.

- He later stopped for coffee and brandy.

- He communicated with the yacht using a walkie-talkie.

- He returned to _Lady Ghislaine_ shortly after 10 p.m.

- Captain Angus Rankin and three crew members greeted him.

- The yacht then departed on Maxwell's instructions.

### 1991-11-05

- Maxwell was last known to have communicated with the yacht's crew during the early morning.

- Later that morning he was discovered to be missing.

- The yacht was cruising off the Canary Islands.

- His body was subsequently recovered from the Atlantic.

The official inquest ultimately attributed the death to a combination of heart problems and accidental drowning, although disagreement existed among pathologists and alternative theories about his death have persisted.

### The mystery of Maxwell's death

The circumstances of Maxwell's disappearance produced enormous speculation.

The published evidence establishes:

- Maxwell was aboard _Lady Ghislaine_.

- The yacht was cruising near the Canary Islands.

- Maxwell disappeared during the early morning.

- His body was recovered from the sea.

- There were no major external injuries reported apart from a graze.

- The official conclusion involved heart failure/heart attack and accidental drowning.

The evidence does not establish that he was murdered.

Ghislaine Maxwell has subsequently said she believes her father was murdered, but that is her belief rather than an established finding.

### What can actually be established

I found reporting that reconstructs portions of the yacht's movements from witnesses, crew accounts, investigators and contemporary journalism.

I did not locate a publicly authenticated complete _Lady Ghislaine_ captain's log covering the yacht's entire period under Robert Maxwell's ownership.

Consequently, it would be misleading to present an invented "ship's log" containing every passenger, port, date and meeting.

The November 1991 reporting is considerably stronger than the evidence available for the yacht's earlier voyages.

## What Is Not Established

The following should not be treated as established merely because they appear in internet discussions:

- a complete passenger manifest for _Lady Ghislaine_

- a complete collection of captain's logs

- a complete list of every party held aboard the yacht

- a complete list of every person who met Robert Maxwell aboard

- evidence that every person photographed aboard was involved in Maxwell's business dealings

- evidence that every political figure present at a party was conducting official business with Maxwell

- evidence that the 1989 party was an intelligence meeting

- evidence that Donald Trump and Robert Maxwell conducted criminal activity aboard the yacht

- evidence that Jeffrey Epstein attended the 1989 yacht party

In particular, Epstein is not listed among the guests in the contemporary 1989 yacht-party reporting I located.

## The 2025 Maxwell interview

The supplied article's central claim concerns Ghislaine Maxwell's interview with Deputy Attorney General Todd Blanche.

The DOJ transcript is important because it permits the exact context to be checked.

### 2025-07-24

- Ghislaine Maxwell was interviewed by Todd Blanche.

- Blanche asked her about when she first knew Donald Trump.

- Maxwell explained that she had been traveling to New York in 1990 while working for her father.

- She said her father had acquired the New York Daily News and was having advertising issues.

- She then said she "may have met" Donald Trump at that time because her father was friendly with him.

- She expressly said she could not remember whether she actually met him in 1990.

- She said this was before she met Jeffrey Epstein.

The wording is therefore more qualified than simply saying "I met Trump in 1990."

The official transcript records Maxwell saying that she did not remember whether she met Trump in 1990.

## Contradict The 2025 Testimony

There is a genuine chronological discrepancy worth noting.

The contemporary 1989 reporting places Maxwell at a yacht party with Donald Trump.

Maxwell's 2025 account says she may have met Trump in 1990 and that she did not remember whether she had actually met him then.

Those statements are difficult to reconcile if the 1989 reporting accurately identifies Ghislaine Maxwell as present with Trump.

However, the evidence does not automatically establish that Maxwell deliberately lied.

There are several logically distinct possibilities:

1. The 1989 report accurately records the meeting and Maxwell's memory was wrong.

2. Maxwell remembered the relationship but misremembered the year.

3. Maxwell deliberately minimized or altered the chronology.

4. The historical report contained an error about a participant.

5. Maxwell's statement was simply an expression of uncertainty rather than a categorical denial.

The available evidence establishes the discrepancy more strongly than it establishes Maxwell's motive.

## Todd Blanche

Todd Blanche is significant because he conducted the 2025 DOJ interview.

The interview transcript shows him asking Maxwell directly about Trump and her relationship with Jeffrey Epstein.

The supplied article argues that Blanche should have recognized the 1989 yacht evidence.

That is an argument/commentary, not an established finding that Blanche intentionally ignored evidence.

The transcript itself shows that Maxwell gave Blanche a qualified answer: she said she may have met Trump in 1990 and did not remember whether she had.

## The Significance Of _Lady Ghislaine_

The yacht is historically important for three separate reasons.

### 1. It was a Maxwell family social venue

It hosted lavish gatherings attended by people from politics, diplomacy, publishing, media and business.

### 2. It was a business platform

Robert Maxwell used the yacht while conducting business, including during his final period of activity in New York and Europe.

### 3. It was the setting of Maxwell's final voyage

The yacht was the last known location associated with Robert Maxwell immediately before his disappearance and death in November 1991.

Those three facts can be established without adopting later theories about intelligence operations, Epstein, or murder.

## Bottom line

The strongest documentary evidence concerning _Lady Ghislaine_ establishes a 1989 New York yacht party attended by Robert Maxwell, Ghislaine Maxwell and Donald Trump, alongside a group of prominent political, diplomatic, media, legal and publishing figures.

It also establishes that Maxwell used the yacht for business and that it was the setting of his final voyage in 1991.

The 2025 DOJ transcript creates a notable chronology issue: Maxwell remembered that she may have met Trump in 1990, while contemporary reporting places her with him aboard her father's yacht in 1989.

That discrepancy warrants scrutiny, but the public evidence located here does not by itself prove intentional perjury or establish that Blanche deliberately overlooked the earlier record.

Most importantly, I found no authenticated complete public ship log or comprehensive passenger manifest that would justify claiming that every meeting, passenger or party aboard _Lady Ghislaine_ has been reconstructed.

Claims that go beyond the documented parties, business encounters and final voyage should therefore be labeled as allegations or speculation.
