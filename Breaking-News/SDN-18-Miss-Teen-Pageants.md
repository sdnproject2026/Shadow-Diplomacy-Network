# Trump's "Miss Teen USA"

In 1996 Trump bought from ITT Corp. the Miss Universe Organization and controlled it until September 2015, when he sold it to WME/IMG, shortly after briefly buying out NBC Universal's 50% stake to gain full ownership amid a public dispute; this spans roughly 19 years.

The Epstein / Trump documented social relationship ran from the late 1980s in West Palm Beach social scenes through at least the early-to-mid 2000s, with Trump claiming a "falling out" around 2004

## Overlap Aligns Perfectly

The peak Epstein / Trump documented social relationship overlap aligns perfectly in years and social circles with Trump's pageant ownership era, including shared events, flights logged in the 1990s, and mutual contacts around young women in modeling/pageant worlds.

## Trump Entering Dressing Rooms

During this time some contestants later described him entering dressing rooms and being closely involved in how young contestants were presented.

The recent “smoking gun” comments from a former pageant judge - Steve Siebold - in whistleblower discussions - argue that a serious investigation into those pageant years - especially by talking to figures like Billy Bush and Nancy O’Dell - could reveal stronger evidence about how Trump and Epstein interacted around young contestants, no proof of specific criminal acts thus far.

# 1972 Elite Model Management

## John Casablancas

Elite Model Management was founded in Paris in 1972 by John Casablancas, a visionary but controversial figure who revolutionized the industry by promoting diverse, high-earning "supermodels".

- Cindy Crawford
- Naomi Campbell
- Linda Evangelista
- Claudia Schiffer

contrasting the prim style of rivals like Ford Models.

## 1983 Look of the Year

Elite pioneered scouting contests like "Look of the Year" (starting 1983), model books/composites, and global expansion with offices in New York (1977), emphasizing rock-star lifestyles over strict etiquette, which sparked the "Model Wars" and generated over $100 million annually by the 1980s.

## 1999 Scandals and Downfall

### Sexual Misconduct

Casablancas faced accusations of sexual misconduct with underage models

- 16-year-old Stephanie Seymour
- drug promotion
- 1999 BBC exposé - offered sex

## 2004 Chapter 11 bankruptcy

Elite filed Chapter 11 bankruptcy in 2004 amid overexpansion and talent loss, later restructuring under new ownership.

## Currently

Today, Elite operates worldwide as a top agency with a focus on individuality and long-term careers, but its legacy is tied to Casablancas' wild era and ethical controversies.

# After-party Meet-and-greet

The after-party following the Miss Universe 2013 pageant on November 9 was hosted at Crocus City Hall in Krasnogorsk, Moscow Oblast, Russia - the same multifunctional concert venue (3rd pavilion of the Crocus City complex) where the main event took place.

## Event Details

Promotional materials and ticket packages explicitly marketed the after-party as a premium perk, promising ticket holders (priced from several hundred dollars upward) exclusive access to meet Donald Trump and mingle with the 86 international contestants in a more intimate setting post-show.

- The venue's flexible design allowed quick reconfiguration from the 7,000+ seat pageant arena into a lounge/party space, leveraging its state-of-the-art acoustics, lighting, and adjacent facilities like the Crocus Expo and Vegas shopping mall for overflow VIP areas.

- Trump was advertised as a draw, with expectations he'd circulate among high-paying guests, Agalarov family members, and select elites.

- reports confirm he attended briefly after the pageant finale.

- Emin Agalarov performed live during the main show and likely stayed for the party, amplifying the family-hosted vibe.

No photos or detailed attendee logs from the after-party have publicly surfaced, but it was framed as a glitzy networking opportunity tying into Trump's real estate ambitions in Russia.
