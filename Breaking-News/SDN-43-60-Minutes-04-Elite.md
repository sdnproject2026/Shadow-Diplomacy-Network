# Elite Model Management

The 1988 60 Minutes Expose

## Corporate Biography

Elite Model Management operated as a globally prominent modeling and talent agency network with major international operations spanning Paris, New York, and other fashion capitals.

Founded and expanded during the latter half of the twentieth century, the agency positioned itself at the pinnacle of the international fashion market, steering the supermodel era and orchestrating transatlantic talent competition.

Because Elite Model Management operated as a privately held entity rather than a publicly traded corporation across its key regional branches during its peak operational eras, comprehensive financial balance sheets, detailed annual reports, and formal board of directors rosters were maintained privately and are not publicly available or verified with one hundred percent certainty in public corporate registries.

### Corporate Structure And Leadership

- John Casablancas and Alain Kittler co-founded the agency in Paris in 1972, establishing a dominant European market presence before expanding operations globally.

- Gérald Marie served as president of Elite Model Management's European branch (Elite Paris) for decades, overseeing high-level booking operations and international fashion capital networks.

- Administrative records, international bookers, and executive management teams directed regional scouting frameworks, connecting European talent pools with North American markets through cross-border capital pipelines.

### Financial Operations And Transparency

- Financial records, revenue streams, and funding structures for Elite Model Management involved private capital arrangements, including historical investment channels tied to executive roles and external funding networks.

- Investigative reports, court testimonies, and legal disclosures revealed that agency leadership generated substantial revenues through international booking commissions, placement fees, and high-stakes model contracts.

- Due to the absence of public financial audits or statutory reporting requirements for private modeling networks of that period, exact annual turnover, profit margins, and cross-border financial conduits cannot be verified with absolute certainty.

### Public Disclosure Requirements for Private Modeling Agencies

Elite Model Management operated as a privately held entity across its key regional branches during its peak operational eras, meaning its internal financial balance sheets, detailed annual reports, and formal board rosters were maintained privately and were not subject to public corporate registration or statutory reporting requirements.

### Impact of Private Ownership on Record Accessibility

Because the agency maintained a private corporate structure, comprehensive financial records, revenue streams, and detailed annual reports remained proprietary documents, preventing full public verification through traditional corporate registries.

### Mechanisms for Managing Cross-Border Contracts

The agency utilized international booking networks, regional scouting frameworks, and executive management teams across Paris, New York, and other global fashion capitals to orchestrate transatlantic talent competition and manage cross-border model placements.
