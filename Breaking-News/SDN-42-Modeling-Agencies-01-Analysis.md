## Epstein Network

Merged And Evidence-Graded Narrative

### Scope And Evidence Grading

This merges article one and article two and fills gaps from searches of major media, international media, court and DOJ records, and Reddit and Substack material.

Every claim carries one of these labels

### [Documented]

- supported by court records, DOJ or congressional records, settlements, or multiple major outlets

### [Alleged]

- claimed by a named accuser, litigant, report, or outlet, and not proven in court

### [Denied]

- the named party denies it

### [Contested]

- credible sources disagree

### [Unverified]

- I could not confirm it in my searches

### [Conjecture]

- my inference, offered where facts run out

## Narrative

### The Core Enterprise

[Documented] Jeffrey Epstein built a decades-long abuse operation around his homes in New York, Palm Beach, the US Virgin Islands, and New Mexico, and around a paid staff and a small circle of facilitators.

- Ghislaine Maxwell was convicted in December 2021 of sex trafficking offenses and is serving 20 years at the federal prison camp in Bryan, Texas, with a projected release in 2037.

- The Second Circuit upheld her conviction in September 2024. The Supreme Court declined her appeal in October 2025.

- Judge Paul Engelmayer denied her habeas petition in an opinion made public on 2026-08-25.

[Documented] The FBI began investigating in 2005 after the parents of a 14-year-old girl reported her molestation at Epstein's Palm Beach home.

- AP reports the FBI identified at least 35 girls with a similar history between 2002 and 2005.

### The Modeling Pipeline

[Documented] Jean-Luc Brunel, a French modeling agent, ran Karin Models and later MC2 Model Management.

- Epstein wired him up to about $1 million to launch MC2, with offices in New York and Miami (some accounts add Tel Aviv).

- Sources date the launch to 2004 or 2005.

- Brunel flew on Epstein's plane on about 25 trips, per Wikipedia's account.

- He was arrested at Charles de Gaulle airport in December 2020 in a French probe of rape and trafficking of minors, and he was found hanged in La Sante prison on 2022-02-19.

[Alleged] A former MC2 bookkeeper told the Daily Beast the agency arranged visas for girls from Eastern Europe and that MC2 girls became frequent guests on Epstein's jets.

- Virginia Giuffre alleged Brunel procured girls, some of them minors, with promises of modeling work.

- Brunel denied wrongdoing through his lawyers, who said his suicide was not driven by guilt.

[Alleged] CBS 60 Minutes investigated Brunel in 1988, and about two dozen women described drugging and rape at his agency.

- A 2022 lawsuit by a California woman against Brunel's estate alleges false imprisonment, attempted drugging, and sexual assault around 2002.

- It does not mention Epstein.

[Documented] The Kyiv Independent found DOJ-released emails with at least two Ukrainian modeling agencies.

- They include a thread in which Brunel discusses a contract and a US trip for a Ukrainian model, and travel arrangements for women from Kyiv and Odesa.

[Documented] A 2019 FBI email discussed contacting Maxwell and nine other alleged co-conspirators, Brunel among them.

[Conjecture] Brunel's visa sponsorship, a financed agency, shared jets, and Epstein's own recruiting history together suggest the modeling world served as a recruitment channel.

- How far the wider industry knew is not established, and article one's industry-wide framing is not supported by any source I found.

### The Zampolli Thread

[Documented] Paolo Zampolli, an Italian-born former modeling agent who founded ID Models in New York, once discussed buying a modeling agency with Epstein.

- He appears several times in the DOJ Epstein files, and he was named a partner in Maxwell's TerraMar Project.

- He later became a Trump special envoy for global partnerships and a Kennedy Center trustee.

[Unverified] The Daily Beast reports he was fired from the envoy role.

- I could not pin the exact date, and Narativ suggests October 2026.

[Denied] Zampolli says he never had a personal relationship with Epstein and calls it "part of the New York scene."

- The Daily Beast notes he has never been accused of participating in Epstein's crimes and that the files do not establish wrongdoing by him.

[Alleged] Amanda Ungaro, his former partner, says she flew from Paris to New York on Epstein's plane in June 2002 at age 17 with Brunel.

- She also says Zampolli was an Epstein associate and "recruiter," and that Melania and Donald Trump have a "pact" with him.

- Zampolli and DHS deny her claims, and the recording was later deleted from her X account.

- The NYT reported in March 2026 that Zampolli asked ICE to detain Ungaro during a custody dispute.

- Zampolli and DHS say no favors were involved.

[Alleged] A 2019 FBI witness summary in the files says Epstein introduced Melania Trump to Donald Trump.

- It also says Zampolli was "trying to buy Elite Models with EPSTEIN."

- This is one witness's account.

- It contradicts Zampolli's long-standing claim that he made the introduction, and Melania Trump and the White House deny it.

[Alleged] Victoria Drake sued Zampolli on 2026-09-30, alleging he sexually assaulted her in 2023.

- He denies it and calls it an extortion effort.

[Unverified] Narativ, a Substack, says the October 26 House Judiciary Democratic report names Zampolli a likely co-conspirator.

- I did not read the report itself.

### Money And Shell Companies

[Documented] Les Wexner gave Epstein broad power of attorney over his finances by 1991.

- Forbes estimates Wexner and Leon Black together accounted for more than 75 percent of Epstein's fee income.

- Black paid Epstein about $158 million for tax and estate planning, and he stepped down as Apollo CEO in 2021 after an outside review.

[Documented] Epstein's main vehicles were Financial Trust Company (relocated to the Virgin Islands) and Southern Trust Company.

- Bloomberg mapped holding shells such as Plan D LLC (jet), Maple Inc. (Manhattan townhouse), and Great St. Jim LLC (island).

- USVI settled with Epstein's estate for at least $105 million over claims that he fraudulently obtained economic-development tax benefits.

- Figures for the benefits vary widely by source, from $73 million and up to about $300 million.

[Alleged] Court documents show former USVI First Lady Cecile de Jongh and other officials tried to soften sex-offender monitoring laws to benefit Epstein.

[Unverified] One secondary source claims more than 30 Epstein-linked shell companies.

- Another claims $1.2-1.5 billion in aggregate bank transfers.

- Both come from non-primary compilations.

[Contested] Whether any of this amounts to criminal money laundering has not been tested.

- I found no criminal money-laundering conviction.

- The bank outcomes below were civil settlements.

### Banks And Enablers

[Documented] JPMorgan was Epstein's bank from 1998 to 2013.

- It settled the Jane Doe class action for $290 million in June 2023 and USVI's suit for $75 million in September 2023.

- Deutsche Bank, his bank from 2013 to 2018, settled for $75 million in May 2023. Black paid USVI $62.5 million in January 2023.

- None of the settlements included admissions of liability. Jes Staley, the former JPMorgan private bank head, settled confidentially.

[Documented] Court documents and depositions show bank employees filed suspicious activity reports on Epstein's large cash withdrawals.

[Alleged] USVI exhibits showed email chains with more concern among JPMorgan legal and compliance staff than previously known.

[Conjecture] This is the best-documented evidence of institutional foreknowledge of at least suspicious conduct.

- It does not by itself show foreknowledge of trafficking.

[Documented] The House Oversight Committee voted on 2026-09-15 to recommend holding Leon Black in contempt of Congress.

- Black's lawyers called it an abomination. I could not confirm a floor vote.

### International Span

[Documented] Epstein had apartments in Paris, and France's Paris prosecutors opened an investigation in August 2019.

- The 2026 file releases triggered action in several countries.

[Documented] Norway charged former prime minister Thorbjorn Jagland with aggravated corruption in February 2026 over gifts, loans, and travel from Epstein.

- He denies wrongdoing. Mona Juul, Norway's ambassador to Jordan, resigned after revelations that Epstein left $10 million in his will to her children.

- She and her husband Terje Rod-Larsen are under investigation.

[Documented] Slovakia's national security adviser Miroslav Lajcak resigned over his emails with Epstein.

- He called them "stupid male egos in action."

[Documented] In Britain, Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor and Peter Mandelson were each arrested in February 2026 on suspicion of misconduct in public office.

- Both are accused of passing confidential government information to Epstein. Mandelson denies wrongdoing, and Andrew denies wrongdoing related to Epstein.

[Unverified] A tracking Substack lists resignations by DP World's Sultan Ahmed bin Sulayem and Paul Weiss chairman Brad Karp.

- I did not confirm these in primary reporting.

[Documented] Ukrainian agency emails (see above) extend the pipeline evidence to Eastern Europe.

- Brunel-linked probes ran in France, and the pageant and agency flights ran Paris to New York.

### Influence Peddling

[Documented] The Epstein files show him cultivating officials in the UK, Norway, Slovakia, the US, and elsewhere.

- NPR and others report that consequences in Europe have been much larger than in the US so far.

[Documented] The DOJ's own OPR review found Acting US Attorney Alex Acosta used poor judgment in the 2007 non-prosecution agreement but did not find professional misconduct.

- The review said the evidence did not show prosecutors agreed to the co-conspirator provision to protect political, celebrity, or other influential associates.

[Alleged] Critics argue the unusual "any potential co-conspirators" immunity clause suggests protection of someone.

[Conjecture] No named beneficiary beyond Maxwell, Sarah Kellen, Nadia Marcinkova, and Lesley Groff has been established.

- The motive for the clause remains unexplained.

[Documented] Several victims allege Epstein gave them to other powerful men for massages that turned sexual.

- A DOJ-released FBI presentation lists such allegations against Leon Black, Harvey Weinstein, and others.

- Black denies wrongdoing, and the Manhattan DA investigated but filed no charges.

### Cover-Up And Foreknowledge

[Documented] The 2007 federal non-prosecution agreement was signed on 2007-09-24 and shielded Epstein, four named co-conspirators, and "any potential co-conspirators."

- Victims were not informed or consulted.

- Epstein pleaded guilty in Florida state court on 2008-06-30.

- A federal prosecutor had drafted a 60-count indictment in 2007.

[Documented] Epstein died in a Manhattan jail in August 2019 and was ruled a suicide by the city medical examiner.

- Brunel died in a similar way in 2022.

[Contested] The administration's statements conflict with FBI records.

- Attorney General Pam Bondi said in February 2025 that Epstein's "client list" was on her desk, but FBI agents wrote that no such list existed.

- House Democrats now allege DOJ concealment.

- The White House says Trump was "fully exonerated."

[Alleged] Critics say not all records were released.

- DOJ says it released nearly 3.5 million pages on 2026-01-30.

[Unverified] A Substack claims 2.5 million more pages are withheld, which I could not verify.

[Conjecture] Foreknowledge probably existed at several levels.

- Maxwell, Brunel, and paid staff almost certainly knew what Epstein was doing.

- Anyone who kept dealing with Epstein after his public 2008 conviction knew he was a convicted sex offender.

- Banks flagged his cash activity.

- None of that shows knowledge of an organized trafficking ring serving others.

### Drug Use Allegations

[Alleged] Some victims say drugs were used to coerce them.

- One FBI-documented victim said she was "lent out" so often that she began using drugs.

- The 1988 60 Minutes report and the 2022 lawsuit allege Brunel drugged women.

[Unverified] Epstein's 2013 draft emails alleging Bill Gates sought drugs after sex with "Russian girls" are unverified allegations Epstein wrote to himself, circulating via Substack summaries.

- Gates has not been charged, and I treat this as unreliable.

[Contested] I found no source documenting "wholesale" drug use across the network.

- The claim is not supported at that scale.

### Trafficking Findings

[Documented] Epstein sexually abused underage girls, and the FBI found ample proof of it.

- Maxwell was convicted of recruiting and abusing minors.

[Contested] The 2026 AP review of internal DOJ files found no corroboration of a "ring" in which Epstein lent girls to powerful men.

- Two other victims Giuffre named said they had no such experience.

- Prosecutors found payments to more than 25 apparent models but no evidence of prostituting women to other men.

- Giuffre's account shifted over time, according to prosecutors.

- The Guardian counters that the 2026 releases contain allegations that Epstein provided victims to other men, which raises questions about officials' claim that there is no basis to investigate third parties.

[Conjecture] The documentary record supports a large abuse enterprise with international recruiting.

- It does not yet support the "wholesale human trafficking network of elites" version.

- The competing reading is that the evidence was never fully pursued, which is plausible given the NPA.

### The October 26 House Judiciary Report

[Documented] On 2026-10-26, eight days before the midterms, House Judiciary Committee Democrats released an unsigned staff report of roughly 50 to 53 pages (sources differ).

- It draws on accounts from 11 women, alleges Trump was an "active participant" in Epstein's abuse of young women in the 1990s, and calls for an independent special counsel and for DOJ records.

- The NYT noted it largely cites allegations already public.

[Alleged] The report includes Beatrice Keul's account that Trump raped her in a Manhattan hotel suite in 1993 after a pageant. I

- BTimes says the allegation is untested in court.

[Unverified] Article one's details on Jill Harth, the Plaza Hotel, and Epstein being introduced as "Don's best friend" were not confirmed in my searches.

[Denied] White House spokeswoman Lauren Bis called the report a "desperate attempt by crazed Democrats to distract Americans" and said Trump was "fully exonerated."

- Article one also credits Steven Cheung with a denial, which I did not confirm.

[Unverified] Article one's claims about Stacey Williams, Lauren Petrella, a promise of NYU admission, and Time Magazine's independent-verification caveat were not checked against the report itself.

### The 2026 Washington Track

[Documented] President Trump signed the Epstein Files Transparency Act on 2025-11-19.

- Democrats won the House on 2026-11-03, and Robert Garcia, expected to chair Oversight, posted "LAWYER UP."

- The new Congress convenes on 2027-01-03. Democrats say they will pursue subpoenas, public hearings, and bank records, and Raskin says law firms with administration deals can expect subpoenas.

[Documented] Article two's investigative tracks (presidential accountability, facilitators, financial institutions, four committees) match this stated agenda.

- Its own standard applies here too.

- Association with Epstein does not establish criminal liability.

# Questions

Which of the people named in the 2007 non-prosecution agreement's "potential co-conspirators" clause, if any, were actually protected by it, and why has that clause never been publicly explained?

What documents would show whether Brunel's visa sponsorships and MC2 payments were used to move girls into the United States, and could French or Ukrainian records fill gaps that US records leave open?

How much of the claimed Epstein shell-company structure and USVI tax benefit scheme was examined by criminal investigators, as opposed to civil plaintiffs, and why did no bank face criminal money-laundering charges?

If Democrats subpoena Epstein-related bank records and DOJ files after January 2027, which allegations in the October 26 report could be corroborated with primary documents, and which rest only on single-witness accounts?

Is the gap between what the FBI found (no ring serving powerful men) and what accusers allege a gap in the evidence, a gap in the investigation, or both?

## Answers

### The NPA's "Co-Conspirator" Clause

- The four named co-conspirators (Maxwell, Sarah Kellen, Nadia Marcinkova, Lesley Groff) are the only people the report establishes as covered. [Documented]

- No beneficiary of the "any potential co-conspirators" language has been identified. [Conjecture]

- The DOJ's OPR review found poor judgment by Acosta but no evidence the clause was meant to shield influential associates. [Documented]

- Critics argue the unusual breadth implies protection of someone, but that remains inference. [Alleged]

- Why it is unexplained: victims were not consulted, the full negotiating record has never been made public, and the OPR review accepted prosecutors' accounts. [Conjecture]

### Brunel's Visas

- The most useful US documents would be visa petitions and sponsor filings (e.g., O-1/H-1B), MC2 bank and wire records, jet flight logs and passenger manifests, and the DOJ-released emails. [Conjecture]

- A former MC2 bookkeeper has already alleged the agency arranged visas for Eastern European girls. [Alleged]

- Epstein wired Brunel up to about $1 million to launch MC2. [Documented]

- French records are the strongest gap-filler: Paris prosecutors opened a probe in 2019, and Brunel's 2020 arrest was tied to a rape and minor-trafficking investigation. [Documented]

- Ukrainian agency emails already show contracts and travel arrangements for women from Kyiv and Odesa; agency-side records there could show who was recruited and how they were paid. [Documented, Conjecture on what they would show]

- Caveat: Brunel's death in 2022 ended any prosecution of him, which limits what French files may ever be released. [Conjecture]

### Shell Companies

- The USVI case was civil. It settled with Epstein's estate for at least $105 million over fraudulently obtained tax benefits. [Documented]

- The bank outcomes (JPMorgan $290M and $75M, Deutsche $75M, Black $62.5M) were all civil settlements with no admission of liability. [Documented]

- I found no criminal money-laundering conviction or charge against any bank. [Contested, untested]

- Claims of 30+ shell companies and $1.2–1.5 billion in transfers come only from secondary compilations. [Unverified]

- Likely reasons for no charges: Epstein died in 2019, suspicious activity reports show banks flagged the activity but not trafficking proceeds, and corporate criminal prosecution is rare. [Conjecture]

### October 26 Report

- Most likely corroborated by primary documents: bank records, flight logs, DOJ and FBI files, and the 2007 NPA record, since these are the Democrats' stated subpoena targets. [Conjecture]

- The NYT noted the report largely cites allegations already public, so it adds little new documentary proof. [Documented]

- Likely single-witness accounts: Beatrice Keul's allegation of a 1993 rape is untested in court. [Alleged]

- The FBI witness summary on the Trump–Melania introduction and Zampolli's Elite Models claim also rest on one source. [Alleged]

- Unchecked: Jill Harth, Stacey Williams, Lauren Petrella and other details were not verified against the report itself. [Unverified]

- Subpoenaed records can show contacts and payments, but rarely prove abuse itself. [Conjecture]

### FBI Findings vs. Accusers' Claims

- Likely both. [Conjecture]

- Evidence gap: the AP review found no corroboration of a ring serving powerful men, two other victims Giuffre named denied such experiences, and prosecutors said her account shifted. [Contested]

- Investigation gap: the 2007 NPA ended federal pursuit early, victims weren't consulted, and the Guardian notes the 2026 releases contain allegations of victims being provided to other men. [Documented, Alleged]

- Records may also be incomplete: critics say not all files were released, though the claim of 2.5 million withheld pages is unverified. [Alleged, Unverified]

- Bottom line: the record supports a large abuse enterprise with international recruiting, but not yet the "elite trafficking network" version. [Conjecture]
