## Legislative Oversight and Enforcement

### Judiciary Committee Hearings

- Democratic Committee Chairs in both the House and Senate are positioned to launch immediate oversight hearings into the DOJ’s non-compliance, blowing of statutory deadlines, and mishandling of data.

### Subpoenaing Internal Logs

- Congress can exercise its subpoena power to demand the DOJ's unredacted logs, internal emails, and directive memos to uncover who authorized the specific redactions that exposed survivors.

### Legislative Remediation

- If court bottlenecks or bureaucratic resistance persist, Congress can leverage its majority to pass an amendment to the Epstein Files Transparency Act
