## Jurisdictional Courtesy

Reform Recommendations

### Defining Jurisdictional Courtesy

- This is an informal, non-binding practice where one law enforcement agency defers its investigation to another (usually a larger or federal agency) to avoid "stepping on toes" or compromising a parallel case.

- It is intended to prevent evidence contamination and redundant work.

### Potential for Abuse

- In the Zorro Ranch case, critics argue this "courtesy" was abused as a "bureaucrative shield."

- By asking the state to stand down without actually performing a federal search, the SDNY effectively created an "investigative vacuum" where no agency was looking at the property.

- This allowed potential physical evidence to sit unexamined for seven years.

### Report Recommendations

- The Truth Commission's final report, due in December 2026, is expected to recommend "Mandatory Notification Laws."

- These would require federal agencies to provide a written "certificate of intent to search" within 90 days if they request a state-level stay; failing this, the state would be legally mandated to resume its own investigation.
