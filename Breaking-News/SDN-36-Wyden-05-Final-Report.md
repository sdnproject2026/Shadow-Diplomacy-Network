# Leon Black Was Overwhelmingly the Largest Source of Funding for Epstein’s Sex Trafficking

As part of this investigation, Senator Wyden’s staff obtained records of a settlement between billionaire Leon Black and the Government of the U.S. Virgin Islands (USVI). These records confirm that Black’s payments to Epstein were a central financial lifeline for Epstein’s criminal operations.

According to the USVI settlement agreement, Black paid Epstein approximately \$170 million over a five‑year period. The settlement explicitly states that Epstein used the money Black paid him to fund his operations in the Virgin Islands, including activities tied directly to Epstein’s sex trafficking enterprise.

## Black’s Payments Were Critical to Epstein’s Criminal Network

The investigation found that:

- Black’s payments dwarfed all other sources of funding Epstein received.  

- Epstein relied heavily on Black’s money to maintain his properties, staff, travel, and the infrastructure used to facilitate sex trafficking.  

- No other individual or entity provided Epstein with comparable financial support.  

The scale of Black’s payments — \$170 million over five years — is unprecedented for purported tax and estate planning services. Epstein was not a credentialed tax advisor, and the payments far exceeded industry norms.

## Epstein’s Dependency on Black’s Money

Epstein used Black’s payments to:

- finance large cash withdrawals  

- pay women and girls  

- fund travel and logistics for trafficking operations  

- maintain properties in the Virgin Islands and elsewhere  

- compensate accomplices and employees who facilitated his criminal activity  

The investigation determined that Black’s payments were not incidental or peripheral. They were the primary financial engine behind Epstein’s operations during the period in question.

## Bank Failures Amplified the Harm

Because Bank of America failed to properly scrutinize or report Black’s payments:

- Epstein was able to move massive sums without triggering timely SAR filings  

- federal law enforcement was deprived of real‑time visibility into Epstein’s financial activity  

- Epstein’s trafficking operations continued uninterrupted for years  

The failure to scrutinize Black’s payments represents one of the most consequential compliance breakdowns uncovered in the investigation.

## Why Black’s Payments Matter

Understanding Black’s role is essential because:

- Epstein’s criminal network required substantial financial resources  

- Black provided the largest and most consistent stream of funding  

- the payments enabled Epstein to operate with impunity  

- banks failed to detect or report the suspicious nature of the transactions  

The investigation concludes that Black’s payments were not only suspicious but were central to Epstein’s ability to sustain his trafficking operations.
