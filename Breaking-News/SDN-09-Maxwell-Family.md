# British Authorities

### 1991

- Following the death of Robert Maxwell in November, Ian and Kevin Maxwell assumed leadership of the family's crumbling business empire.

### 1992

- In June 1992, Ian and Kevin Maxwell were arrested in the United Kingdom by the Serious Fraud Office (SFO).

### 1995

- The "trial of the century" for the Maxwell brothers took place in London at the Old Bailey.

- In January 1996, both Ian and Kevin Maxwell were acquitted of conspiracy to defraud.

- Throughout this period, there is no public record of direct personal interaction or collaborative investigative work between the Maxwell brothers and William Barr, who had concluded his first term as US Attorney General in early 1993.
