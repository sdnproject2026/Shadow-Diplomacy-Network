## AI Limitation - the “all” question

_Blanche-specific EFTA productions_ above from primary DOJ documents and indexed copies, but I cannot responsibly claim that these are every individual EFTA number in the entire 3.5-million-page release that happens to contain the words “Todd Blanche.”

The DOJ repository contains _12 Data Sets and millions of pages_, and DOJ does not publish a person-to-EFTA cross-reference index. The individual EFTA numbers are consequently much harder to exhaustively enumerate than the 12 Data Sets themselves.

The independent Epstein Exposed index currently says Blanche appears in _156 case documents_, but its public person page does not expose all 156 EFTA identifiers in a form that permits me to verify every number against DOJ's primary files. It does, however, independently confirm the Maxwell proffer agreement and the Blanche connection.

## Important Blanche-related EFTA's

The July 2025 Maxwell interview material is especially significant because Blanche was not merely mentioned in the records: _he personally conducted the DOJ interview_.

The DOJ's own transcript identifies Blanche throughout the interview. The first-day transcript includes questions about Epstein's relationships, the alleged “black book,” prominent individuals, Epstein's properties and Maxwell's knowledge of Epstein's activities. The second-day transcript continues the questioning.

This is also corroborated by congressional and secondary-source material.

A House Oversight transcript of Pam Bondi's testimony says that

> Todd Blanche supervised the Epstein Files Transparency Act release process

and Bondi testified that she spoke with the Deputy Attorney General practically every day about the process.

Separately, Public Citizen's July 2026 research timeline identifies Blanche's July 24–25 Maxwell interviews and links the underlying DOJ transcripts, while documenting the broader sequence of Blanche's involvement in the Epstein-files controversy.

## DOJ Data Sets versus EFTA batches

It would be misleading to say, for example, “Data Set 6 is the Blanche batch.”

The DOJ's _Data Set 1–12_ structure is a repository organization system, whereas the EFTA numbers identify individual productions.

DOJ itself describes the releases as twelve Data Sets, with individual files such as EFTA00000001.pdf, EFTA02730265.pdf, etc.

Secondary reporting also explains that the Data Sets function more like large volumes/chapters and that their contents overlap rather than constituting clean chronological or subject-matter divisions.

Therefore, for research purposes I recommend retaining the _EFTA number as the primary identifier_, and recording the DOJ Data Set separately whenever its mapping has been independently established.
