# JEp Repository

## justice.gov

https://www.justice.gov/epstein 

Official DOJ Epstein Library: Central repository for court records, DOJ disclosures, and other files from the Epstein case, including scanned images, audio, and some OCR'ed documents.

- [1][2][3]

## epstein-docs.github.io

https://epstein-docs.github.io/all-documents

Epstein Archive: Community-driven archive with scanned and OCR'ed documents, organized for public access and searchability.

- [11]

## journaliststudio.google.com

https://journaliststudio.google.com/pinpoint/search?collection=092314e384a58618  

Pinpoint: Searchable database of over 20,000 documents from Epstein's estate, including text, images, and OCR'ed scanned files.

# Transcripts, Audio Recordings

## archive.org/stream

- https://archive.org/stream/epsteindocs/12%23%20Epstein%20deposition's%20-%20Edwards%20vs%20Epstein%20+%20attachments_djvu.txt  

Internet Archive collection with Epstein deposition transcripts, audio recordings, and related documents, including plain text transcripts of audio and video evidence.
- [1]

## rev.com/transcripts
- https://www.rev.com/transcripts/900-epstein-documents-unsealed-transcript  

Rev.com hosts transcripts of over 900 unsealed Epstein court documents, including depositions and other audio/video evidence converted to plain text.
- [2]

## archive.org/stream
- https://archive.org/download/epstein-flight-logs-unredacted-17  

Internet Archive directory with unredacted Epstein flight logs, including plain text and searchable transcripts of audio/video evidence.
- [3]

## archive.org/details

- https://archive.org/details/epstein-docs_20240108  

Internet Archive repository containing a large collection of Epstein-related documents, with plain text transcripts of audio and video files.
- [4]

## justice.gov

- https://www.justice.gov/epstein/doj-disclosures  

Official DOJ disclosures, including BOP video footage and related transcripts, available for download.
- [5]
