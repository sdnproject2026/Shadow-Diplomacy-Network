# Epstein’s Aides Oversaw Movement of Cash and Should Be Investigated

Jeffrey Epstein relied on a small group of trusted aides to move large amounts of cash around the world. These individuals played key operational roles in sustaining Epstein’s trafficking network by handling logistics, finances and day‑to‑day management of his accounts and entities.

## Key Aides Involved in Cash Movement

Three former Epstein employees were central to the movement of funds:

- **Darren Indyke**  

- **Richard Kahn**  

- **Harry Beller**

These aides were responsible for:

- coordinating cash withdrawals  

- transferring funds between Epstein‑controlled entities  

- wiring money to women and girls  

- managing payments to accomplices  

- facilitating transactions through foreign jurisdictions  

- maintaining Epstein’s financial infrastructure across multiple banks  

Their involvement was not peripheral. They were essential operators who ensured Epstein’s financial network functioned smoothly.

## Role in Epstein’s Operations

Evidence reviewed by investigators shows that these aides:

- executed transactions that lacked legitimate business purpose  

- moved cash in patterns consistent with human trafficking indicators  

- handled payments to individuals in high‑risk jurisdictions  

- maintained accounts used to obscure the source and purpose of funds  

- coordinated with Epstein’s bankers to process large withdrawals  

Their actions enabled Epstein to:

- pay victims  

- fund travel and logistics  

- maintain properties used for trafficking  

- compensate employees who facilitated criminal activity  

## Why These Aides Must Be Investigated

The investigation determined that Indyke, Kahn and Beller:

- had direct knowledge of Epstein’s financial activity  

- were involved in transactions flagged as suspicious  

- played operational roles in moving money used for trafficking  

- acted as intermediaries between Epstein and financial institutions  

Given their involvement, federal prosecutors and financial regulators should conduct thorough investigations into:

- their handling of Epstein’s accounts  

- their role in facilitating suspicious transactions  

- their knowledge of Epstein’s criminal activity  

- whether they violated federal anti‑money‑laundering laws  

## Summary

Epstein’s aides were not passive employees. They were active participants in the movement of cash that financed Epstein’s sex trafficking operations. Their conduct merits investigation by federal authorities to determine the full extent of their involvement and potential criminal liability.
