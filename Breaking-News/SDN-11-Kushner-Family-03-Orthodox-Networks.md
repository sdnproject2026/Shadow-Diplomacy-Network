## Joseph Kushner Hebrew Academy

The Joseph Kushner Hebrew Academy (JKHA), located in Livingston, New Jersey, serves as a cornerstone of the Kushner family’s philanthropic and communal legacy.

Named in honor of the family patriarch, the school is frequently cited not just as an educational institution, but as a nexus for high-net-worth individuals within the Orthodox Jewish community and the broader real estate and political landscape of the New York metropolitan area.

Below are 10+ prominent individuals associated with the academy as trustees, major donors, or high-profile alumni.

### Charles Kushner

> Founder, Real Estate Mogul, and Patriarch of the Kushner Family

- As the primary benefactor who established the school in his father's name, Charles remains the central figure in its financial history.

- His influence shaped the school into a prominent communal hub.

### Jared Kushner

> Alumnus, Former Senior Advisor to the President, and Investor

- A graduate of the academy, Jared’s rise to global political and financial prominence has historically brought significant attention and high-level networking opportunities to the institution.

### Joshua Kushner

> Alumnus and Founder of Thrive Capital

- Also an alumnus, Joshua is the family’s first billionaire.

- His success in venture capital and his marriage to supermodel Karlie Kloss have expanded the school’s association with the global elite.

### Dara (Kushner) Orbach

> Trustee and Philanthropist

- Serving on the Board of Trustees, Dara is deeply involved in the school's governance.

- She is a key figure in maintaining the family’s hands-on relationship with the academy’s daily operations.

### Murray Kushner

- The brother of Charles Kushner, Murray is a major real estate developer in his own right.

- His role as an Honorary Trustee signifies the broader family’s institutional support.

### Jeremy Halpern

- A principal at Atlantic Realty, Halpern leads the school's governance.

### Jeffrey Lichtman

### Sandy Steiner

> President of the Academy

- Steiner provides executive leadership for the institution, managing the interface between the school's high-net-worth donor base and its academic administration.

# Academy Affiliations

## 1978

### Rishon LeZion Partnership Initiation

- Established a relationship with Israel's fourth-largest city through Project Renewal.

- Initial focus on the disadvantaged neighborhood of Ramat Eliyahu (now Neve Eliyahu), which has evolved into a city-wide connection.

## 1996

### Expansion of International Partnerships

- Formed a partnership with Cherkassy, Ukraine, in collaboration with the American Jewish Joint Distribution Committee (JDC).

- Integrated the Ofakim/Merchavim region into the Federation’s network through the Jewish Agency for Israel (JAFI) Partnership 2000 program.

## 2006

### Hurfeish Connection

- Established a unique bond with the Druze village of Hurfeish in northern Galilee following the Second Lebanon War.

- Marks the only U.S. Federation partnership connecting a Diaspora Jewish community to an Israeli Arab village, supporting Druze citizens who serve in the IDF.
