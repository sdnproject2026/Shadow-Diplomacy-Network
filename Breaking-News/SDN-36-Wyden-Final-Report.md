### Verified Facts of the Case

- On August 4, 2026, Senator Wyden officially published a 67-page congressional report titled “Looking the Other Way: How Wall Street Banks Enabled Jeffrey Epstein’s Sex Trafficking”.

### The Core Evidence

- The findings are based on a four-year review of internal financial corporate records, legal filings, and nonpublic Treasury Department Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs).

### The Primary Violations

- The investigation uncovered that Bank of America, JPMorgan Chase, and Deutsche Bank routinely allowed millions of dollars in highly irregular cash withdrawals and wire transfers to go unflagged for years, potentially in direct violation of the Bank Secrecy Act.

### The Corporate Responses

- Responses from the implicated banks have been mixed. For instance, representatives for Bank of America publically stated that the bank did not intentionally facilitate any wrongdoing, while others failed to provide a formal record of comment.

The full primary source documentation detailing this investigation is public and available directly through the U.S. Senate Finance Committee Portal.
