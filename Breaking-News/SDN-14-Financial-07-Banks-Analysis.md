## Important Limitations

- The Dataset 10 GitHub report lists 14 Jes Staley mentions, but that figure describes a particular dataset and search methodology rather than all DOJ EFTA records.

- A separate GitHub investigation reports approximately 7,250 corpus pages referencing Staley. Page counts and distinct-document counts are different measures and cannot be substituted for each other.

- The claim that Staley appears in 4,743 records, attributed to a Reddit post and an independent website, has not been independently reproduced against the underlying DOJ archive.

- The `LMSBAND/epstein-files-db` project explicitly states that its dataset is incomplete.

- The `rhowardstone/Epstein-research` project provides useful record-level research, EFTA identifiers, and Dataset 10 analysis, but its published counts must be interpreted according to each report's coverage and methodology.

- A zero in an exact-name search would mean only that the specified query returned no matches in that dataset; it would not establish that a person never appears in the complete archive.

- Search results may miss spelling variants, aliases, OCR errors, scanned records, and duplicate files.

- The numeric value 14 for Jes Staley is a repository-reported dataset-specific figure. The remaining NA values indicate that comparable counts have not been established; they are not zero counts.

- The figure of 4,743 associated with Staley comes from a Reddit post and related independent reporting, but has not been reproduced against the complete DOJ archive. The approximately 7,250 figure concerns corpus pages and is not directly comparable to the 4,743-record claim or the Dataset 10 count of 14. Averaging these figures would produce a misleading result because they measure different units and may cover different datasets.

- Counts for related individuals are retained only where a source provides a relevant numeric result. Association counts, name-variant counts, and distinct-record counts are not interchangeable.

- Mentions do not establish misconduct or criminal liability.

- The additional primary-person entries above use repository-specific counts when available. They should not be interpreted as independent verification of every person's relationship with Epstein or with another named individual.

- The available Dataset 10 report identifies some names with zero exact-name matches, but those results are not equivalent to a full-text, archive-wide search. Its own methodology notes that the redaction-layer results substantially underestimate full-text presence.
