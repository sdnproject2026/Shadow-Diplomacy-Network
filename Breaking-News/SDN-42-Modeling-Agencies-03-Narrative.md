# Modeling Agencies

## Narrative

The global modeling industry operates as a high-stakes ecosystem of glamour, corporate power, and fierce competition. Spanning fashion capitals like New York and Paris to burgeoning creative hubs in Lagos and São Paulo, the landscape is steered by veteran executives, sharp-eyed scouts, and multi-industry power players.

In the global fashion capitals of 2020-2025, a vast web of leaders, executives, and associates steered the world's most prominent modeling agencies.

## Summary

At the helm of WME Fashion and IMG Models, Susan Plagemann, Ivan Bart, and Mimi Yapor worked alongside high-profile associates like Ari Emanuel, Mark Shapiro, Richard Weitz, Luis Domingo, and Ryan Dye. Meanwhile, at Elite Model Management—which navigated industry shifts around 2019—Gabriel Stones and Christophe Devue collaborated with Kelly Jang, Taylor Avery, and Paolo Barbieri.

Over at Ford Models, Michael Douglas and Nicholas Dolan led the charge with associates Henry Ford III, Jim Baumbick, and Katie Ford, while Wilhelmina Models was guided by Sean Patterson and Hunter Singh, supported by Ginni Conquest, Polina Reed, and Bill Wackermann. Next Management featured Kyle Hagler and Alexis Borges working alongside Faith Kates, Joel Wilkenfeld, and Tina Clark.

DNA Model Management relied on Roseann Rustici and Tony Craig, alongside associates Jerome Bonnouvrier, David Bonnouvrier, and Eileen Kim. At Women Management, Jennifer Powell shared leadership with Paolo Barbieri (from Elite World Group), alongside Julia Haart, Tanni Foreman, and Alan Foreman. The Society Management was steered by Chris Gay and Taylor Avery, joined by Jon Ilani, Elizabeth Mitchell, and Sarah Boyd. The Lions featured Julia Kisla Taylor and Ali Kavoussi working with iconic names like Frida Aasen, Michelle Alves, and Alessandra Ambrósio. Marilyn Agency rounded out the New York scene with Marilyn Gauthier, Francine Champagne, Alexander Kaelin, Anissa Jenness, and Pam Warner.

Expanding across Europe and the globe, Spain established its footprint with Uno Models led by Javier Macías and Michel Jouneau alongside Fernando Merino, and Sight Management Studio directed by Eduardo Sayas with associate Irene. In Nigeria, Beth Model Management was founded by Elizabeth Elohor Isiorho (alongside Elohor Isiorho), working with Jahn Affah and Mayowa Nicholas. In Brazil, Ford Models Brazil was guided by Guerman Aliev and Barbara K., with associates Luisa Rosa, Bela Zinato, and Denise Cespedes, while Mega Model Brasil was run by Sabrina Muller, Eliandro Rocha, Marcus Panisset, and Eli Hadid.

European hubs thrived with Sergio Barbagallo and Cyril Bromet at Viva Model Management in France, Sarah Doukas and Simon Chambers leading Storm Management in the UK alongside Kate Moss, and Maurizio Mansi directing Why Not Model Management in Italy with Alessia Ghisletta. Together, this intricate network of leaders shaped the international modeling landscape through decades of evolution.

## Networking

### 1. IMG Models (United States)

As an absolute titan in the industry, IMG Models operates under WME Fashion (part of Endeavor). The narrative of IMG is deeply intertwined with corporate reach and elite talent cultivation. Under the executive leadership of President Ivan Bart, President of WME Fashion Susan Plagemann, and Vice President Mimi Yapor, the agency handles some of the world's most defining faces and inclusive advocates. Behind them stand high-level corporate associates and executives like Ari Emanuel, Mark Shapiro, Richard Weitz, Luis Domingo, and Ryan Dye, charting the intersection of fashion, sports, and entertainment.

> ~1,000 to 1,500+ across its global hubs (New York, Paris, Milan, London, Sydney).

### 2. Elite Model Management (United States)

Founded in Paris in 1972, Elite is historic royalty in the modeling ecosystem. Led by Chief Executive Officer Gabriel Stones and Head of Legal Christophe Devue, alongside associates like Kelly Jang, Taylor Avery, and Paolo Barbieri, Elite has weathered decades of industry evolution, legal shifts, and cultural change while maintaining a formidable global footprint across multiple continents.

> ~2,000+ models globally through its network of international affiliates.

### 3. Ford Models (United States)

A foundational pillar of the American modeling industry, Ford Models was established decades ago and continues to shape trends globally. Guided by Chief Executive Officer Michael Douglas and Chief Legal Officer Nicholas Dolan, alongside a legacy line of associates including Henry Ford III, Jim Baumbick, and Katie Ford, the agency balances heritage with modern digital and commercial placement.

> ~400 to 500 active talents globally (spanning its main NYC hub, Paris, Los Angeles, Chicago, and Miami).

### 4. Wilhelmina Models (United States)

Famed for discovering iconic talent and maintaining a massive commercial and editorial presence, Wilhelmina is steered by President Sean Patterson and Executive Vice President Hunter Singh. Key associates such as Ginni Conquest, Polina Reed, and veteran executive Bill Wackermann anchor its diverse boards spanning fashion, fitness, and curve representation.

> ~800 to 1,000 models across its primary US and international boards.

### 5. Next Management (United States)

Next Management is renowned for discovering avant-garde fashion stars and high-fashion editorial icons. President Kyle Hagler and Agency Director Alexis Borges lead the charge, supported by industry heavyweights and long-standing associates like Faith Kates, Joel Wilkenfeld, and Tina Clark.

> ~600 to 900 models across major fashion capitals.

### 6. DNA Model Management (United States)

DNA is famously selective, maintaining a tight roster of high-impact editorial superstars and runway exclusives. The agency is directed by Tony Craig alongside Chief Financial Officer Roseann Rustici, with key figures like Jerome Bonnouvrier, David Bonnouvrier, and Eileen Kim shaping its prestigious reputation.

> ~150 to 300 highly curated elite models (deliberately smaller to focus on top-tier campaigns).

### 7. Women Management (United States)

Women Management has long served as a powerhouse for defining editorial looks and launching legendary supers. Director Jennifer Powell leads the leadership team, working alongside Elite World Group CEO Paolo Barbieri and associates Julia Haart, Tanni Foreman, and Alan Foreman.

> ~400 to 600 models across its New York and Paris offices.

### 8. The Society Management (United States)

Launched as an offshoot to handle elite high-fashion talent with hyper-focused management, The Society is helmed by CEO Chris Gay and Director of Scouting and Development Taylor Avery, with key operational support from associates Jon Ilani, Elizabeth Mitchell, and Sarah Boyd.

> ~200 to 350 elite high-fashion models.

### 9. The Lions (United States)

Known for a boutique, relationship-driven approach to management, The Lions represents major global supermodels and high-profile personalities. The agency is led by CEO Kisla Taylor and Managing Partner Ali Kavoussi, with a unique roster presence reinforced by prominent model associates such as Frida Aasen, Michelle Alves, and Alessandra Ambrósio.

> ~100 to 200 select high-profile talents and models.

### 10. Marilyn Agency (United States)

With deep roots in both New York and Paris, Marilyn Agency was built on classic elegance and high-fashion scouting. Founder & CEO Marilyn Gauthier and Owner & President Francine Champagne lead the agency, alongside associates Alexander Kaelin, Anissa Jenness, and Pam Warner.

> ~200 to 300 models.

## International Markets

### 11. Beth Model Management (Nigeria)

Pioneering the path for African fashion onto the global stage, Beth Model Management is spearheaded by founder Elizabeth Elohor Isiorho. Operating out of Lagos, it acts as the premier launchpad for African talent—most notably discovering international superstar Mayowa Nicholas. Alongside Isiorho, key team members and associates like Jahn Affah and Mayowa Nicholas drive its mission.

> ~100 to 200 developing and internationally placed models.

### 12. Ford Models Brazil (Brazil)

Carrying the legacy of the Ford name into South America’s vibrant creative core, Ford Models Brazil is steered by Chairman Guerman Aliev and Executive Director Barbara K., with core booking leadership from associates like Luisa Rosa, Bela Zinato, and Denise Cespedes.

> ~300 to 500 models across the Brazilian market.

### 13. Mega Model Brasil (Brazil)

As one of Latin America's largest and most aggressive commercial and fashion scouting agencies, Mega Model Brasil is led by Director Sabrina Muller alongside a senior administrative and booking team, with key figures including Eliandro Rocha, Marcus Panisset, and Eli Hadid.

> ~500 to 800+ commercial and fashion models.

## FR - France

### 14. Viva Model Management (France)

Operating as one of Paris’s premier high-fashion boutique powerhouses, Viva Model Management is led by Founder Sergio Barbagallo alongside key booking leadership including Cyril Bromet.

> ~200 to 400 high-fashion and editorial models.

### 15. Elite Model Management Paris (France)

Serving as the foundational centerpiece of the global Elite network, Elite Paris dominates the French market under Senior Director & President leadership across runway, commercial, and editorial divisions.

> ~450 to 700+ fashion and runway models.

## GB - United Kingdom

### 16. Storm Management (United Kingdom)

Established as a cornerstone of London’s fashion ecosystem, Storm Management is guided by Founder Sarah Doukas and Managing Director Simon Chambers, famously discovering and managing iconic global talent.

> ~300 to 600 commercial and high-fashion models.

### 17. Select Model Management London (United Kingdom)

Recognized as a leading innovator in global model scouting, Select London operates under Executive Chairman Matteo Puglisi and senior booking directors across high-fashion and main board representation.

> ~400 to 700 talent and fashion models.

## IT - Italy

### 18. Why Not Model Management (Italy)

As one of Milan’s long-standing premier fashion agencies, Why Not Model Management is spearheaded by Managing Director Maurizio Mansi and senior agency associates including Alessia Ghisletta.

> ~250 to 450 luxury and editorial models.

### 19. d'management group (Italy)

Positioned at the epicenter of Milanese luxury and international runway casting, d'management is steered by Founder & Managing Director David Brown alongside key booking directors managing top-tier global talent.

> ~350 to 600 high-fashion models.
