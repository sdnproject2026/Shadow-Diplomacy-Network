# Epstein Victim Litigation

Known lawyers representing Jeffrey Epstein victims in federal and state courts for civil and criminal cases include prominent attorneys who handled high-profile suits against Epstein's estate, Ghislaine Maxwell, and enablers.

## 2019

### David Boies

Boies Schiller Flexner LLP  

Represented victims like Teresa Helm, Annie Farmer, Maria Farmer, Juliette Bryant in federal civil suits against Epstein's estate in Manhattan for rape, battery, false imprisonment; also suits against banks like JPMorgan and Deutsche Bank.

## 2019

### Gloria Allred  

Represented Jane Doe 15 and Teala Davies in federal court suits in Southern District of New York against Epstein's estate alleging trafficking and abuse starting in 2004 and 2002.

## 2019

### Jordan Merson  

Filed federal civil suit in New York on behalf of Jane Does 1-9 against Epstein's estate for battery, assault, emotional distress from 1985-2000s abuses.

## 2024

### Sigrid McCawley

Boies Schiller Flexner  

Key lawyer in federal civil suits for victims like Virginia Giuffre against Prince Andrew and Maxwell-related defendants; ongoing Epstein-related litigation.

## Ongoing

### James Marsh

Marsh Law Firm

### Robert Glassman
