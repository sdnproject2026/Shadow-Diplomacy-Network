## Missed Deadline

### October 1, 2026

Yes, this is true today.

Journalist and trial attorney Katie Phang released a legal update confirming that Attorney General Todd Blanche missed the October 1, 2026, deadline set by U.S. District Judge Emmet Sullivan.

Instead of filing the required certified list of the contents of the Administrative Record, Blanche filed a last-minute motion to postpone the deadline. 

The core details of the current development include:

- The Argument: Blanche's team explicitly argued that compiling the requested list by the court's deadline is "not a good use of the Court's time or taxpayer resources." 

- The Delayed Materials: The administrative record includes all underlying materials the government relied upon under the Epstein Files Transparency Act, which Phang's lawsuit argues is being used to hide unredacted files—including handwritten FBI interview notes mentioning Donald Trump. 

- The Stakes: Judge Sullivan previously ordered that the certified list be handed over by October 1, followed by the complete record on October 15. 

- Because Blanche has pushed back against these dates, Phang and her legal team argue this is an ongoing stall tactic to escape accountability. 

- Judge Sullivan has already placed the DOJ on notice for potential civil contempt charges if they continue to disregard his explicit timelines.
