# Bondi - Wiles

## Overview

Pam Bondi and Susie Wiles are longtime Republican political figures whose careers intersect primarily through Florida Republican politics, Donald Trump's political network, and their association with Ballard Partners.

They are not known to have had a formal supervisor-subordinate relationship.

Their connection is primarily professional and political, developed through overlapping work in Florida Republican circles and later through Trump's campaigns and administration.

## Pam Bondi

### Education

Attended University of South Florida

Earned a Bachelor of Arts degree from University of Florida

Earned a Juris Doctor degree from Stetson University College of Law

### Employment

Prosecutor

- Worked as a prosecutor in the Hillsborough County State Attorney's Office

- Served for many years as an assistant state attorney handling criminal cases in Florida

Florida Attorney General

- Elected as the 37th Attorney General of Florida

- Served from 2011-2019 under Governor Rick Scott

- Became one of the highest-profile Republican state attorneys general in the United States

Trump Political Activities

- Supported Donald Trump during the 2016 presidential campaign

- Served on Trump's legal and political teams during various periods following his presidency

- Participated in Trump-related political and legal advocacy efforts

Lobbying And Consulting

- Joined Ballard Partners after leaving office as Florida attorney general

- Worked as a lobbyist and consultant representing corporate and governmental clients according to federal lobbying disclosures and Senate materials

US Attorney General

- Nominated by President Trump and served as US

- Attorney General beginning in 2025 according to available records

Notable Activities

- Prosecutorial work in Florida criminal justice matters

- Leadership of multistate legal actions while Florida attorney general

- High-profile surrogate and defender of Trump

- Lobbying and public affairs work after leaving Florida office

- Service as US Attorney General in Trump's second administration according to available records

## Susie Wiles

### Education

Graduated from University of Maryland, College Park

Sources describe her degree as being in English and completed in the late 1970s

### Employment

Early Republican Political Work

- Worked for Jack Kemp

- Participated in the 1980 presidential campaign of Ronald Reagan

- Worked in scheduling and advance operations associated with the Reagan administration

Florida Government And Local Politics

- Worked in Jacksonville political and municipal government circles

- Served in the administration of John Delaney

- Served as Delaney's chief of staff from 1997-2000

- Later worked in the administration of John Peyton

Campaign Strategist

- Managed the successful 2010 gubernatorial campaign of Rick Scott

- Worked briefly on the presidential campaign of Jon Huntsman Jr.

- Became a major Republican strategist in Florida politics

Trump Campaigns

- Served as a senior Florida strategist for Trump's 2016 campaign

- Helped manage Trump's Florida operations in 2016

- Played major roles in Trump's 2020 and 2024 political efforts

- Co-managed the 2024 Trump campaign with Chris LaCivita

- Widely credited by supporters with helping run a more disciplined 2024 campaign

Lobbying And Consulting

- Worked with Ballard Partners after Trump's 2016 election victory

- Also worked with public affairs and consulting organizations including Mercury Public Affairs according to multiple biographical accounts

White House Chief Of Staff

- Appointed White House Chief of Staff in January 2025

- Became the first woman to hold that position in US history according to multiple biographical sources

Notable Activities

- Reagan campaign and White House work

- Jacksonville municipal government leadership

- Rick Scott gubernatorial campaign victory

- Ron DeSantis gubernatorial campaign management in 2018

Leadership of Trump's 2024 campaign

- Service as White House Chief of Staff

## Pam Bondi And Susie Wiles

### Shared Florida Republican Network

- Both became prominent figures in Florida Republican politics during the rise of the state's modern Republican establishment

- Both worked closely with political figures such as Rick Scott

- Both developed extensive connections within Florida campaign, fundraising, and policy circles

### Shared Trump Orbit

- Both became trusted allies of Donald Trump

- Bondi often operated in legal, policy, and public advocacy roles

- Wiles primarily operated as a strategist, campaign manager, and organizational leader

- Their influence came through different channels but within the same political network surrounding Trump

### Ballard Partners Connection

- Both have been employed by or associated with Ballard Partners

- The firm became especially influential after Trump's 2016 election

- Their shared affiliation with Ballard Partners represents one of the clearest direct professional links between them

### Trump Administration 

- Wiles became White House Chief of Staff in Trump's second administration

- Bondi served as Attorney General in that administration

- This placed them among the most senior and influential officials in Trump's governing team

- Publicly available information does not establish a close personal relationship beyond their longstanding professional and political connections within the Trump and Florida Republican networks

### Key Similarities

- Long careers in Republican politics

- Strong ties to Florida political networks

- Trusted by Donald Trump

- Employment ties to Ballard Partners

- Senior roles in Trump's political and governmental operations

- Significant influence behind major Republican electoral efforts in Florida and nationally

## Opinions

### Primary Sources

Reuters reporting generally describes Wiles as one of the most effective political strategists in Trump's orbit and credits her with imposing greater campaign discipline during the 2024 election cycle

Britannica characterizes Wiles as a key architect of multiple Republican victories and a central figure in Trump's return to office

### Secondary Sources

Reddit discussions frequently portray Wiles as a behind-the-scenes power broker who exercised significant influence over campaign operations and White House access, though such commentary reflects user opinion rather than verified fact

Reddit commentary regarding Bondi often focuses on her lobbying activities and Trump loyalty, but these discussions should be treated as opinion rather than established historical evidence
