# Khashoggi Family

## A Subplot "Soap Opera"?

While Adnan became famous as a high-flying billionaire and international arms dealer, his father provided the foundational influence and access that made such a career possible.

## The Father-Son Dynamic

### Dr. Mohammad Khashoggi 

(The Insider)

A surgeon of Turkish descent, he served as the personal physician to King Abdulaziz Al Saud, the founder of modern Saudi Arabia.

This position made him a trusted member of the royal inner circle, giving his family proximity to power long before the Kingdom became a global oil superpower.

### Adnan Khashoggi 

(The Fixer)

Using the connections and capital provided by his father, Adnan transitioned from a student in the US to a global intermediary.

He famously leveraged a $10,000 gift from his father - intended for a car - to instead purchase trucks that he leased to a construction company, marking his first major deal.

## Extended Family

The Khashoggi family tree is notably intertwined with several major historical figures and events.

### Nephews

- Jamal Khashoggi (see Washington Post)
- Dodi Fayed (see Princess Diana)

Adnan was the uncle of Jamal Khashoggi (the assassinated journalist) and Dodi Fayed (who died in the 1997 car crash with Princess Diana).

### Sisters

- Samira Khashoggi (see Harrods)
- Soheir Khashoggi (novelist)

His sister Samira Khashoggi married Mohamed Al-Fayed (former owner of Harrods), while his sister Soheir Khashoggi is a renowned novelist.

## Mohamed father of Dodi Fayed

Dodi was Mohamed's eldest child and the only child from his first marriage to Samira Khashoggi, the sister of billionaire arms dealer Adnan Khashoggi.

### The Matchmaker

Mohamed is widely credited with orchestrating the 1997 romance between Dodi and Diana, Princess of Wales, by inviting the Princess and her sons to his villa and yacht in the South of France.

### Professional Ties

- Allied Stars - film production company  
    - produced Chariots of Fire 
- Marketing for Harrods

Dodi worked within his father's business empire, notably serving as an executive for the family’s film production company, Allied Stars (which produced Chariots of Fire), and assisting with marketing for Harrods.

### The Tragedy

- Alleged British Conspiracy

Following the fatal car crash in Paris on August 31, 1997, Mohamed spent much of the remainder of his life funding private investigations and promoting theories that the deaths of Dodi and Diana were not an accident but a conspiracy by the British establishment.

## 1950s Merger

The 1950s merger between the Al-Fayed and Khashoggi families created one of the most powerful and influential private business alliances in the Middle East.

### Egyptian and Saudi  

This union effectively combined Egyptian entrepreneurial drive with Saudi royal access.

### The Patriarch

- Dr. Mohammad Khashoggi

Role

- Personal Physician to King Abdulaziz Al Saud.

Contribution

- Provided the "Insider" royal access and initial capital that allowed the next generation to launch global ventures.

The Nexus

- Samira Khashoggi & Mohamed Al-Fayed

Relation

- Samira (daughter of Dr. Mohammad) married Mohamed Al-Fayed in 1954.

- This marriage transformed Al-Fayed from a salesman in Alexandria into a key player in Saudi commerce.

- He was hired by his brother-in-law, Adnan Khashoggi, to help manage the family’s emerging import and shipping businesses.

Family

- Dodi Fayed (born 1955), who became the living embodiment of the combined family interests.

### The Intermediary

- Adnan Khashoggi

Role

- Brother of Samira and brother-in-law to Mohamed.

Activity

- During the mid-1950s, Adnan and Mohamed operated together under the Al Nasr Trading Company.

- Adnan utilized Mohamed's logistical talent to fulfill the Saudi Kingdom's massive infrastructure needs, while Mohamed utilized the Khashoggi name to bypass traditional trade barriers.

### The Extended Network

- The Yassin Connection

Relation

- After divorcing Mohamed Al-Fayed in 1956, Samira married Anas Yassin, a high-ranking Saudi Ambassador.

Impact

- This secondary union further cemented the family's "Insider" status within the Saudi diplomatic corps, while Mohamed Al-Fayed leveraged the capital and connections gained during the marriage to launch his own independent shipping empire in Dubai and London.

## 1956 Divorce

According to the unsealed 2026 Special Products files and intelligence dossiers, the 1956 divorce between Samira Khashoggi and Mohamed Al-Fayed was not merely a domestic separation but a high-stakes financial decoupling that nearly dismantled the Al Nasr Trading Company.

# Dodi Fayed's Uncles

Based on the information available in the search results, Dodi Fayed's uncles from both his mother's and father's sides are:

## Maternal Uncles 

(Family of Samira Khashoggi)

- Dodi’s mother, Samira Khashoggi, was the daughter of Muhammad Khashoggi.

- Adnan Khashoggi is a well-known Saudi Arabian businessman and billionaire arms dealer.

- He was Samira’s brother and was instrumental in Dodi’s father's early business career.

## Paternal Uncles 

(Family of Mohamed Al-Fayed)

- Dodi’s father, Mohamed Al-Fayed, was the eldest of five children.

- Ali Fayed is a brother of Mohamed Al-Fayed.

- He worked with Mohamed in their shipping agency, Fayed and Co., in Egypt before they moved their interests to Europe.

- Salah Fayed is another brother of Mohamed Al-Fayed who was also involved in the family's shipping business during its early years in Alexandria.

## Related Family

While they are cousins rather than uncles, the late journalist Jamal Khashoggi was a first cousin of Dodi Fayed (their fathers were brothers, or in this case, Jamal's father was the brother of Dodi's uncle Adnan and mother Samira).
