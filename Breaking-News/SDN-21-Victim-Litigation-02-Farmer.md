# Missing" 1996 FBI Files

The case of the "missing" 1996 FBI file represents a pivotal shift in the pursuit of institutional accountability. For nearly three decades, the narrative suggested that early warnings had simply fallen through the cracks. 

The 2025 discovery changed everything, transforming a story of negligence into an allegation of deliberate concealment. 

The core of the contention is the discovery of the 1996 log, which serves as a "smoking gun" for the plaintiffs. 

It shifts the argument from "the FBI didn't know" to "the FBI knew and chose not to act." 

The lawsuit argues that if the FBI had followed standard protocols in 1996, the "Epstein Pilot" would have been grounded before the vast majority of his victims were even targeted.

## Dates

### 1990s
* Maria Farmer and Annie Farmer are allegedly abused by Jeffrey Epstein at his properties.

### 1996 - 09 - 03
* Maria Farmer officially files a detailed complaint with the FBI.
* The complaint includes descriptions of a surveillance system and photographic evidence.
* FBI agents log the complaint and assign it an official file number.

### 1996 - 2005
* The FBI internal file remains inactive and undisclosed to other law enforcement agencies.
* Jeffrey Epstein continues to recruit and abuse dozens of additional victims.

### 2005
* The Palm Beach Police Department initiates an investigation into Epstein.
* The 1996 Farmer report is not provided to local Florida investigators.

### 2025
* A hidden internal FBI document surfaces confirming the 1996 report was officially logged.
* The discovery contradicts decades of claims that no record of the warning existed.

### 2026
* 12 women file a $100 million lawsuit against the FBI for the intentional burial of evidence.

---

### The Buried Warning

* The 1996 file was not a vague tip; it was a comprehensive roadmap that could have dismantled the Epstein enterprise in its infancy.
* According to the lawsuit, the document contains specific details that were ignored for 29 years including the surveillance network, the mansion operations, and the systemic failure.
* The log shows the report was classified in a way that should have triggered a mandatory investigation into interstate sex trafficking.

### The 100 Million Dollar Claim

* The plaintiffs argue that the FBI’s silence was not just a mistake - it was a catalyst.
* The system didn't just fail; it actively shielded the truth.
* If that file is acted upon in 1996, the next 20 years of victims never happen.
