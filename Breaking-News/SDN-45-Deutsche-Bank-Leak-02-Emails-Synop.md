# Deutsche Bank Leak 

The article is designed to provide an organized reference list and verification status of email contacts, publication sources, and investigative bylines associated with the global media coverage of the "Inside Deutsche Bank" document leak.

## Synopsis

This text is a technical, directory-style reference document designed for researchers, investigative journalists, or investigators tracking media coverage of the Deutsche Bank leak. Its primary purpose is to consolidate disparate contact information into a structured, verified directory to assist in identifying appropriate outreach channels for media inquiries.

### Verification and Methodology Notes

- The document begins by defining the scope of the contact list, explaining how 100 email addresses were organized into four sets (`IMEDIA01` through `IMEDIA04`). It explicitly states its methodology regarding data deduplication, the distinction between "retained" (previously supplied) and "newly verified" contacts, and the limitations regarding independent whistleblower security.

### Contact Directory by Media Outlet

- The core of the document is a comprehensive `Sources and domain contact directory`. This section is organized alphabetically by publisher domain (e.g., `forbes.com`, `zdfheute.de`, `db.com`). For each domain, it includes:

   -  Verified Resources: Official contact pages, editorial email addresses, and media inquiry desks.

   -  Byline/Author Context: Links to relevant investigation articles and credits for reporters involved in the consortium.

   -  Verification Status: Clear notes explaining whether an address is an official editorial line, a corporate response contact, or if a specific byline/email remains unverified.

### Consortium and Subject Attribution

- The text distinguishes between independent news organizations and the central investigation hub, `inside-db.com`. It identifies key project roles, such as Data Custodians and Investigative Directors, and provides specific corporate contact information for `db.com` (Deutsche Bank), framing these as media relations responses rather than editorial tip channels.

### Formatting and Structural Layout

- The document utilizes a hierarchical structure:

   -  Numbered Lists: Used to group bulk emails into manageable sets.

   -  Heading Hierarchy: Uses top-level headings for the main title, second-level headings for contact sets, and third-level headings for specific domain names to allow for quick navigation.

   -  Bulleted Notes: Provide context on the validity or purpose of specific emails (e.g., distinguishing between "subscription" and "editorial" lines).

### Scope and Limitations

- The document consistently notes where information is limited, such as when a specific author's email could not be verified or when an article's individual byline was not retrieved. It emphasizes that retention of a contact does not imply suitability for confidential news tips.

## Summary

In short, this article explores and presents a verified contact directory for the "Inside Deutsche Bank" media coverage by organizing media outlets into a systematic, domain-based reference guide, making specific editorial and corporate contacts clear and accessible for professional follow-up.
