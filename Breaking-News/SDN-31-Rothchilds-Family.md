# Paris headquarters Raid

On March 24, 2026, the French National Financial Prosecutor's Office (PNF) confirmed that a raid was conducted at the Paris headquarters of the Swiss bank Edmond de Rothschild. While social media headlines frequently link this to "child sex trafficking," official judicial statements clarify that the search is part of a preliminary investigation into passive corruption and complicity involving a former employee.

## 2026-01-30

- The U.S. Department of Justice releases millions of pages of "Epstein Files" under new transparency laws.

- Revelations begin to surface regarding the "French Connection," specifically naming Fabrice Aidan and Jack Lang.

## 2026-02-04

- French media outlets Le Monde and Mediapart publish detailed reports on Ariane de Rothschild's 2013-2019 correspondence with Epstein.

- Reports include emails where Epstein compares women to "shrimp" and discusses an aquarium in the Caribbean.

## 2026-02-16

- French police raid the Arab World Institute in Paris.

- Jack Lang resigns as president of the institute following the opening of a tax fraud investigation linked to the Epstein revelations.

## 2026-03-20

- Investigators from the PNF and the central office against corruption conduct the search at the Edmond de Rothschild offices in Paris.

- The bank confirms it is "cooperating fully" with the judicial authorities.

## 2026-03-24

- The National Financial Prosecutor's Office (PNF) formally confirms the raid to the press.

- Legal proceedings are clarified as an investigation into passive corruption of a foreign public official (Aidan).

## Opinions

### Primary Sources (NYT, Reuters, AP, Le Monde)

Mainstream reporting maintains a cautious, factual tone. The consensus among these outlets is that while the "sensational" aspects of the sex trafficking ring are the backdrop, the actual legal jeopardy for the bank and individuals like Aidan centers on financial corruption, tax fraud, and the mishandling of state secrets. Editorials in Le Monde suggest this is a "reckoning" for the French diplomatic elite who operated with impunity in Epstein’s orbit.

### Secondary Sources (Reddit, X, Mediapart)

Online commentary is significantly more aggressive. On platforms like Reddit, users frequently conflate the financial raid with the broader child trafficking allegations, often expressing skepticism that the PNF will actually prosecute the "higher-ups" in the Rothschild family. Investigative site Mediapart has been more critical of the bank's shifting narrative, pointing out that the bank initially denied Ariane de Rothschild had ever met Epstein before being forced to retract that statement.
