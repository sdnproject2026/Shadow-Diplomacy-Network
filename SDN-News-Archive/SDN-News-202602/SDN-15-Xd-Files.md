# Cease And Desist Notices

In early February 2026, the Department of Justice (DOJ) faced a significant legal and administrative crisis after releasing millions of documents with inadequate redactions.

### February 1, 2026

The DOJ issues emergency "Takedown Notices" to primary cloud-hosting platforms, including Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Google Cloud, specifically targeting unofficial mirrors that had scraped the Epstein Library directory within minutes of the January 30 release.

This effort specifically targets sites in jurisdictions where the Epstein Files Transparency Act (EFTA) does not provide a legal shield for the publication of PII.

### February 6, 2026
