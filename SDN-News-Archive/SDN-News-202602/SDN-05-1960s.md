# 1960s Shadow Diplomacy

## Transitional Era

In the context of the 2026 unsealed records and other sources, the 1960s emerge as the era when the network's foundational members transitioned into roles of significant public and clandestine influence.

## Donald Barr

### Academic Administration

1959–1964

- Served as the assistant dean of the faculty at Columbia University’s School of Engineering.

- He was tasked with administering the Cooperative College-School Program for the National Science Foundation.

### Appointment to Headmaster

1964

- Became the headmaster of the Dalton School in Manhattan.

- He established a "no-nonsense," traditionalist curriculum that prioritized high academic standards and strict discipline.

### Governmental Intersection

1960s

- During this period, Barr’s work at Columbia and Dalton began to align with the "intellectual recruitment" pipelines often utilized by the burgeoning postwar intelligence community.

## Robert Maxwell

### Member of Parliament

1964–1970

- Elected as a Labour Party MP for Buckingham.

- During his tenure, he was a vocal advocate for technological and scientific advancement but was frequently criticized for his "shady" business practices.

### Pergamon Press 

1960s

- He built Pergamon Press into a global leader in scientific, technical, and medical (STM) publishing, aggressively acquiring trade journals and technical book titles.

### Loss of Control

1969

- Following intense corporate infighting and allegations of financial misrepresentation, Maxwell was temporarily ousted from the board of Pergamon, a precursor to the systemic fraud allegations that defined his later career.

## Adnan Khashoggi

### Arms Brokering Entry

1962

- Began his career as a middleman during the Yemen Civil War, helping supply weapons to Saudi-backed forces.

### Agency Partnerships

1960s

- He became the primary Saudi agent for Rolls Royce, Marconi, and the British Aircraft Corporation (BAC).

### Six-Day War of 1967

1960s

- Established his global conglomerate, which spanning five continents, focused on bridging Western defense contractors (Lockheed, Raytheon, Northrop) with Saudi oil wealth following the Six-Day War of 1967.

## Leslie Wexner

### Founding of The Limited

1963

- After leaving his parents' clothing store, Wexner borrowed $5,000 from his aunt to open a specialty shop in Columbus, Ohio, focusing exclusively on women’s sportswear.

### Business Model Innovation

1960s

- He pioneered the "specialty retail" concept, focusing on a limited selection of high-turnover items rather than the broad inventory of traditional department stores.

### Whirlwind Expansion

1969

- He prepared for an IPO and began the aggressive scaling that would later lead to the acquisition of major brands.

## Alan "Ace" Greenberg

### Bear Stearns

1960s

- Having joined as a clerk in 1949, Greenberg spent the 1960s ascending the firm's hierarchy.

### Profitable Decades

1960s

- Gained a reputation on Wall Street for "frugality and risk-taking," a duality that defined the culture of Bear Stearns during its most profitable decades.

### Senior Figure

1969

- By 1969, he was already a senior figure responsible for recruiting the next generation of aggressive traders, including his eventual successor, James Cayne.

## Edgar Bronfman, Sr.

### President of Seagram

1957–1971

- Throughout the 1960s, Bronfman oversaw 81% of the Seagram Company's business as the head of its American subsidiary, Joseph E. Seagram & Sons.

- He significantly increased the range of Seagram products and improved international distribution channels.

### MGM Investment

1966–1969

- Led the family investment firm, Cemp Investments, to purchase a major stake in MGM, briefly serving as the studio’s chairman in 1969.

## Jean-Luc Brunel

### Early Life

1946

- Born in 1946 in Paris

### Restaurants

1960s

- Brunel spent the late 1960s in the restaurant public relations sector before pivoting toward the burgeoning fashion and modeling world of Paris.

## Ari Ben-Menashe

### Youth & Migration

1960s

- Spent the early 1960s in Tehran, Iran, attending the American Community School.

### Move to Israel

1966

- At age 14, he moved with his mother to Israel, enrolling in the American International School in Kfar Shmaryahu, setting the stage for his eventual entry into the IDF Signals Intelligence in the early 1970s.
