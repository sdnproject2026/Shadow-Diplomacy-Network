# Hills and Kushner EB-5

The intersection of Carla Hills’ trade advisory and the Kushner family’s financial ventures lies at the nexus of high-level diplomatic policy and private-sector capital acquisition.

While Carla Hills established the macro-economic and regulatory "rules of the road," the Kushner family utilized those same pathways for large-scale international investments.

The relationship between Carla Hills’ work on the US-China Business Council and the Kushner family’s EB-5 funding is defined by the synchronization of trade policy and real estate financing.

## Strategic Advocacy 

(Macro-level)

As Chair of the US-China Business Council, Carla Hills advocated for stable bilateral trade and the free flow of capital between the US and China.

This advocacy helped sustain the legal and diplomatic environment where Chinese high-net-worth individuals felt secure investing in US assets.

## The EB-5 Pipeline 

(Micro-level)

The Kushner family utilized the EB-5 "Green Card for Investment" program to secure low-interest mezzanine financing for major projects like One Journal Square.

These deals often targeted Chinese investors who were encouraged by the very trade stability that the US-China Business Council promoted.

### Financial Details

The Kushner family sought approximately $150 million in EB-5 funding for their New Jersey developments.

This capital was often sought at interest rates significantly lower than traditional commercial loans (frequently as low as 1-2%), made possible by the "safe haven" status of the US real estate market - a status bolstered by the trade frameworks Hills spent decades maintaining.

## Hills & Affinity Partners 

The role of Hills & Company in the operations of Jared Kushner’s Affinity Partners is less about direct collaboration and more about the institutionalization of the sovereign wealth investment protocols that Kushner eventually utilized.

### Institutional Foundations

Roderick Hills, through his chairmanship of the SEC and later advisory work at Hills & Company, was a key architect of the anti-corruption and disclosure standards for foreign capital.

These standards created the "compliance infrastructure" that allowed sovereign wealth funds (SWFs) to move massive sums into the US without triggering immediate national security blocks.

### Affinity Partners Funding

In 2021, Jared Kushner’s Affinity Partners secured a $2 billion commitment from the Saudi Public Investment Fund (PIF).

This was followed by an additional $1.5 billion from Qatari and Emirati funds by late 2024, bringing total assets under management (AUM) to approximately $4.8 billion to $5.4 billion by 2025.

### Financial Details

The protocols established by the Hills for transparent cross-border deals inform the fee structures reported by Affinity.

Kushner's firm reportedly collects a 1.25% management fee on the Saudi $2 billion (approx.

$25 million annually) and nearly 2% from other international investors.

As of late 2024, the firm had collected an estimated $157 million in fees without yet returning realized profits to its sovereign investors.

### Regulatory Navigation

The "political risk assessment" model pioneered by Hills & Company is a cornerstone of how Affinity Partners operates.

The firm acts as a strategic intermediary, often navigating CFIUS (Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States) reviews - a process where Carla Hills’ previous cabinet-level experience set the historical precedents for what constitutes "national interest" in foreign-backed deals.
