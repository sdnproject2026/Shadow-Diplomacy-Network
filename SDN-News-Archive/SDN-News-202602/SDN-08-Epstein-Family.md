# Karyna Shuliak

Karyna Shuliak is a Belarusian-born dentist who became a long‑time girlfriend and close associate of Jeffrey Epstein, receiving extensive financial and immigration support from him and later emerging as a major beneficiary of his trust.

 - https://www.nytimes.com/2026/02/16/nyregion/columbia-epstein-dental-shuliak.html

## Early life

Karyna Shuliak was born in 1989 in Belarus and initially studied at Belarusian State Medical University, where she began training in dentistry before leaving the country. She later moved to the United States in her early twenties, by 2009–2011, after forming a relationship with Jeffrey Epstein while living in Minsk. 

- https://www.standard.co.uk/news/world/karyna-shuliak-jeffrey-epstein-will-b1269923.html


## Relationship with Epstein

By around 2011, Shuliak had become a close associate and romantic partner of Epstein and moved to the United States under his sponsorship. The Justice Department documents and released correspondence depict a deeply personal relationship, with Shuliak expressing affection and Epstein actively coaching her on how to secure US entry and legal status. 

- https://www.nytimes.com/2026/02/03/business/jeffrey-epstein-trust-inherit-karyna-shuliak.html


## Education and Columbia dentistry

Epstein arranged for Shuliak to attend the Columbia University College of Dental Medicine, where she ultimately studied dentistry between roughly 2013 and 2015. Records show that Epstein paid her full dental-school costs—over 600,000 dollars in tuition and related support—and also made at least 50,000 dollars in donations to Columbia in her name while influential faculty helped secure her admission after an initial rejection. 

- https://news.bloomberglaw.com/white-collar-and-criminal-law/how-jeffrey-epstein-and-an-nyc-dentist-got-his-girlfriend-into-columbia


## Professional career

After graduating from Columbia’s dental program in 2015, Shuliak obtained a US dental license with Epstein’s help and practiced as a dentist. She at one point owned or worked at a dental practice on St. Thomas in the US Virgin Islands, near Epstein’s private island, before later being reported to reside and work in New York.

Epstein arranged legal and immigration assistance for Shuliak, including hiring a New York–based immigration lawyer to help her obtain permanent residence and, eventually, US citizenship in 2018. Reporting also indicates that, amid visa concerns in 2013, she entered a marriage with an American woman, obtained a green card and then citizenship, and later divorced, with the arrangement described in coverage as part of Epstein’s broader pattern of using sham marriages and visas to keep women in the US

Released US Justice Department records portray Shuliak as a central figure in Epstein’s final years, managing aspects of his schedule and inner circle and at times being described as an “inspector” overseeing his affairs. # Scott Borgerson

Ghislaine Maxwell reportedly entered into a secretive marriage with maritime‑technology entrepreneur Scott Borgerson in the mid‑2010s, a relationship that was kept from most of her family and the public and only surfaced through court filings during her U.S. prosecution.

## Timeline Of The Relationship

Reports trace Maxwell and Borgerson’s relationship to about 2013, when they appeared together at Arctic and ocean‑conservation conferences and he joined the board of her marine‑conservation nonprofit, the TerraMar Project.

Media accounts say Borgerson was still married to his first wife until their 2015 divorce, which she reportedly sought after discovering his affair with Maxwell.

## Secret Marriage

Multiple outlets and court‑document summaries describe Maxwell and Borgerson as having secretly married around 2016, not 2012, with no public marriage record ever located.

In a 2020 bail application, Maxwell’s lawyers told the court she was married but did not name her spouse; later reporting, and her family’s comments, identified Borgerson as that unnamed husband and noted that even her siblings had not known of the marriage until then.

## Reasons For Secrecy

The relationship and marriage were kept intensely private, with prosecutors noting that Maxwell refused to disclose her spouse’s identity or finances to pretrial services.

Commentators have suggested the secrecy served to shield Borgerson from public scrutiny tied to Maxwell’s association with Jeffrey Epstein, and to preserve flexibility in how assets were structured between them.

## Financial Intertwining

Bail‑hearing materials indicated that Maxwell had transferred substantial assets to a trust controlled by her husband, and later filings described the couple as having shared a “committed” household life from their marriage until shortly before her 2020 arrest.

Reports also note that by 2019–2020, roughly 22 million dollars in combined assets were largely held in Borgerson’s name, complicating efforts to trace Maxwell‑linked wealth during her criminal case.

## End of Marriage

Accounts from court proceedings and subsequent reporting state that the relationship effectively ended while Maxwell was in custody, with Borgerson allegedly telling her on a prison phone call that he had moved on and was seeing someone else.

Sources close to her told journalists that she had tried to initiate divorce proceedings so that her husband would not suffer reputational damage from being associated with her prosecution, and later summaries describe the marriage as having ended by 2021.

## Additional Details

In addition to the details provided regarding Scott Borgerson’s relationship with Ghislaine Maxwell, further information concerning his professional background, legal entanglements, and personal timeline has surfaced through media investigations and subsequent court proceedings.

### 2020

# Harvard University Report
