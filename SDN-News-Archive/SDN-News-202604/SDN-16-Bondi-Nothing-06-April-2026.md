## Congressional Enforcement Mechanisms

### Criminal Contempt Of Congress

The House can vote to hold a witness in criminal contempt. This is then referred to the DOJ, specifically the U.S. Attorney for the District of Columbia.

### Civil Contempt

The House can file a civil lawsuit in federal court seeking a declaratory judgment that the subpoena is valid and an injunction requiring Bondi to comply.

### Inherent Contempt

The House has the authority to have its Sergeant-at-Arms take a person into custody to compel testimony. In today's legal climate, this is considered a extreme option and is rarely pursued.

## Civil Law And Disclosure Obligations

### The Epstein Files Transparency Act

This law creates a statutory duty for the DOJ to release documents. If the department failed to comply with the specific timelines or redaction rules, they could face civil litigation from victims' groups or news organizations.

### Individual Liability

Generally, high-level officials are protected by _qualified immunity_ from personal civil liability for actions taken within their job duties. However, this does not shield them from being called as a witness in civil cases.

# History

## 2026-03-05

- The House Oversight and Government Reform Committee voted to subpoena former Attorney General Pam Bondi to answer questions about the Justice Department's handling of Jeffrey Epstein related files.

    - https://www.cnn.com/2026/03/17/politics/pam-bondi-subpoena-house-comer-epstein

- The subpoena set a deposition date for 2026-04-14.

    - https://www.cnn.com/2026/03/17/politics/pam-bondi-subpoena-house-comer-epstein

## 2026-04-02

- President Donald Trump fired Pam Bondi as attorney general.

    - https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-fires-pam-bondi-us-attorney-general-cnn-fox-2026-04-02/

- That later became the central dispute over whether the subpoena still applied to her after she left office.

    - https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/bondi-wont-appear-for-house-deposition-next-week-in-the-epstein-investigation

## 2026-04-07 - 2026-04-08

- The Justice Department told the committee that Bondi would not appear because she had been subpoenaed in her official capacity as attorney general and no longer held that office.

    - https://www.cbsnews.com/news/bondi-deposition-subpoena-house-oversight-committee-epstein/

- Committee staff said they would contact Bondi's personal counsel to discuss next steps and rescheduling.

    - https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/08/jeffrey-epstein-pam-bondi-house-oversight.html

- Democrats on the committee said the subpoena remained binding on Bondi personally and warned they could move toward contempt if she did not comply.

    - https://abcnews.com/Politics/doj-house-oversights-subpoena-longer-obligates-bondi-testimony/story?id=131841587

## 2026-04-14

- Bondi did not appear for the scheduled deposition, creating the reported no-show.

    - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R8GhHEez-hQ

- House Oversight said it was working to reschedule the deposition.

    - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R8GhHEez-hQ

- The no-show intensified the legal dispute over whether a congressional subpoena survives a change in office for a person named individually in the subpoena versus summoned in an official capacity.

    - https://www.co-equal.org/case-law-precedents/civil-enforcement-of-congressional-subpoenas

## Legal Requirements And Civil Law

- Congressional subpoena enforcement can lead to contempt proceedings, including criminal contempt referrals to the Justice Department or civil enforcement in court.

    - https://federal-lawyer.com/subpoenas/congressional-subpoena/enforceable/

- Reuters reported that ignoring a congressional subpoena can expose a witness to contempt of Congress procedures and possible criminal penalties under the 1857 statute, while courts have also recognized Congress's investigative authority.

    - https://www.reuters.com/world/us/whats-stake-trump-allies-facing-contempt-congress-2021-10-14/

- In the Bondi dispute, one side argued the subpoena became moot because it targeted her as attorney general, while the other side argued it remained binding because it was directed to Pam Bondi by name.

    - https://www.cbsnews.com/news/bondi-deposition-subpoena-house-oversight-committee-epstein/

## Consequences

- The immediate consequence of the no-show was delay in the Oversight Committee's Epstein files inquiry and a likely rescheduling effort.

    - https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/bondi-wont-appear-for-house-deposition-next-week-in-the-epstein-investigation

- The political consequence was sharper partisan conflict, with Democrats framing the absence as defiance and Republicans arguing the subpoena no longer applied in the same way after she left office.

    - https://abcnews.com/Politics/doj-house-oversights-subpoena-longer-obligates-bondi-testimony/story?id=131841587

- The legal consequence could be contempt proceedings if the committee chooses to escalate, though civil or criminal enforcement would depend on the chamber's next steps and the Justice Department's response.

    - https://www.reuters.com/article/world/how-hard-hitting-are-us-congress-subpoenas-contempt-citations-idUSKCN1SD0HA/
