## Andrew and Tristan Tate

Andrew and Tristan Tate are dual US–British internet personalities who were arrested by US Marshals in Miami, Florida on July 18, 2026, following an extradition request from the United Kingdom. The arrest took place just days after the brothers attended a private Washington, DC gathering called "Zampoli's Summer Soiree".

### The DC Visit and "Zampoli's Soiree"

### The Event

- On July 15, 2026, the brothers were guests of Paolo Zampolli, Trump's Special Envoy for Global Partnerships, at a 150-person summer gathering hosted at his DC residence.

### Other Sightings

- During their mid-July trip to the capital, they were seen on Capitol Hill meeting with lawmakers, including Texas Representative Wesley Hunt, and were photographed at The Ned, an exclusive private club.

### Social Fallout

- The party sparked international friction when Romania's foreign minister, Oana Toiu, abruptly left the event upon realizing the Tate brothers—who previously faced human trafficking indictments in Romania—were in attendance.

## Current Legal Status and Charges

The Tate brothers remain in federal custody in the US while facing extradition proceedings to the UK.

### The UK Warrant

- The Crown Prosecution Service leveled 38 new charges against the brothers, which includes allegations of rape, sex trafficking, assault, and possession of indecent images of a child. This brings their total number of global criminal charges to 59.

### Extradition Stance

- While the brothers' legal team publicly appealed to President Trump to block the extradition—claiming the charges are a "political witch hunt"—reports indicate the administration will not block the transfer to the UK once a federal judge confirms it complies with treaty laws.
