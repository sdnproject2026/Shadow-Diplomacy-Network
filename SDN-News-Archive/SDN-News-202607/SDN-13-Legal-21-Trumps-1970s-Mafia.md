  - Gravano, Salvatore - Underboss

  ### Synopsis

  The Gambino family has long been involved in various organized crime activities, notably in construction fraud and waste disposal contracts. They exerted substantial influence over construction projects in New York City, often leveraging connections to secure lucrative contracts illegally.

  - Convictions

    - Gravano, Salvatore - 1994

## Colombo Family

### Members

  - Persico, Carmine - Boss

  - Basciano, Vincent - Capo

  - Gallo, Joseph - Associate

  ### Synopsis

  The Colombo family has frequently targeted the construction industry, engaging in schemes such as fraud and extortion. They have been known to infiltrate waste disposal businesses to exploit profits and maintain control over certain areas of operation.

  - Convictions

    - Persico, Carmine - 1986

    - Basciano, Vincent - 2006

## Lucchese Family

### Members

  - Corallo, Anthony "Tony Ducks" - Boss

  - Scaturro, Frank - Associate

  - DiVeglio, Anthony - Underboss

  ### Synopsis

  The Lucchese family has a reputation for being heavily involved in construction and labor racketeering. They have historically manipulated labor unions to control the workforce in construction projects, ensuring their members benefit financially from these ventures.

  - Convictions

    - Corallo, Anthony - 1986

    - Scaturro, Frank - 2002

## Bonanno Family

### Members

  - Massino, Vincent - Boss

  - Galante, Carmine - Capo

  - Cacace, John - Associate

  ### Synopsis

  The Bonanno family has primarily focused on infiltrating the waste management industry and maintaining control over various casinos. Their activities often included extorting funds from legitimate businesses and exerting influence through intimidation and corruption.

  - Convictions
