# Capital One Closes Accounts

## 100+ Trump Bank Accounts

March 2021

## Timeline

### 2021 - 01 - 06

- The U.S. Capitol riot occurs, intensifying scrutiny on organizations and financial accounts tied to Donald Trump.

### 2021 - 03

- Capital One formally notifies the Trump Organization and Eric Trump that it will terminate their corporate banking relationships.

- Capital One closes more than 300 bank accounts affiliated with the Trump Organization.

### 2025 - 03

- The Trump Organization and Eric Trump file a lawsuit against Capital One in a Miami federal court.

- The lawsuit alleges political discrimination and "woke" debanking practices motivated by the January 6 Capitol events.

### 2025 - 08

- The Trump administration issues an executive order prohibiting financial institutions from discriminatory debanking based on political ideologies. [1] 

### 2026 - 07 - 31

- Capital One files a federal court motion to dismiss the latest amended version of the Trump Organization's lawsuit.

- The bank's response publicly reveals that an internal anti-money laundering (AML) review, rather than politics, drove the account closures.

## Opinions

### Primary

New York Times

- Frame the conflict as part of a highly regulated post-January 6 landscape where major financial firms aggressively offloaded high-risk political clients to avoid regulatory penalties.

Bloomberg

- Highlight the legal precedent established by the filing, emphasizing that compliance teams are legally protected when dropping clients based on standard AML risk matrices.

Reuters

- Note that Capital One’s explicit clarification that no crime was committed serves as a strategic move to undermine defamation claims while preserving the bank's right to manage risk.

### Secondary

Reddit (r/politics)

- View the revelation as confirmation that the business operations of the Trump Organization inherently carry extreme financial and institutional risks.

Reddit (r/Conservative)

- Interpret the bank's AML defense as a coordinated legal cover story designed to hide politically motivated discrimination against conservative figures.

WallStreetOasis

- Argue that the closure of 300 accounts simultaneously is a standard corporate compliance reaction when transaction patterns become too complex to audit cost-effectively.
