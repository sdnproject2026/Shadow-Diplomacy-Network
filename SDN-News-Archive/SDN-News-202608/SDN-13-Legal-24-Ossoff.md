# Senator Jon Ossoff

He Integrated the Jeffrey Epstein files into his broader political platform, transforming a long-standing public records controversy into a central pillar of his legislative and campaign messaging.

## 2025

The Push for Full Disclosure

### July 12, 2025

- Speaking at a rally in Savannah, Georgia, Senator Ossoff explicitly injects the Epstein files into the state's political discourse. He sharply criticizes President Donald Trump for failing to make the records public despite previous populist transparency promises.

### July 15, 2025

- Capitalizing on mounting legislative pressure, lawmakers introduce the bipartisan Epstein Files Transparency Act (H.R. 4405) to force the Department of Justice (DOJ) to publish its full investigative backlog.

### November 18–19, 2025

- Under immense bipartisan scrutiny and pressure from Senate Democrats, the transparency bill sails through the House and passes the Senate by unanimous consent. Facing significant blowback from his base, President Trump signs it into law.

### December 19, 2025

- The executive branch begins its formal compliance with the new law, with the DOJ releasing the first massive data sets of investigative records to the public.

## 2026

Coining "The Epstein Class" on the Campaign Trail

### January 30, 2026

- The DOJ finishes publicizing its final primary tranches of declassified files, uncovering logs, emails, and law enforcement files detailing high-profile elite associations.

### February 7, 2026

- At a massive election rally in Atlanta, Ossoff launches a viral campaign message, explicitly labeling Trump's ultra-wealthy cabinet and allies as "the Epstein class". He frames the MAGA platform as a populist contradiction—accusing billionaires of cutting rural clinics and safety nets under the guise of fighting the establishment.

### February 19, 2026

- Ossoff takes this messaging to national television during an appearance on The Late Show with Stephen Colbert, leaning on his background as a former international corruption investigator to make the case that these elite networks operate under a separate set of rules.

### June 1, 2026

- Heading into the heat of the Georgia Senate race, Ossoff intensifies his attacks at an election rally alongside Keisha Lance Bottoms.

- He heavily mocks local Republican contenders as establishment insiders protecting a corrupt administration, keeping the Epstein oversight battle at the very center of his 2026 re-election strategy.

### June 17, 2026

- Sen. Jon Ossoff's campaign released a television advertisement attacking Republican Senate candidate Mike Collins over the Epstein files.

- The advertisement argued Collins voted against disclosure legislation and accused him of protecting powerful elites.

### June 18, 2026

- PolitiFact published a fact-check concluding Ossoff's campaign advertisement omitted important context regarding Mike Collins' remarks about Donald Trump and the Epstein files.
