# Pam Bondi Impeachment

Representative Summer Lee (PA-12) officially introduced four articles of impeachment against Bondi, citing a "sweeping pattern of abuses of power."

The charges primarily focus on Bondi’s defiance of the Epstein Files Transparency Act and her refusal to comply with a House Oversight Committee subpoena for unredacted documents related to the Jeffrey Epstein investigation.

The articles of impeachment specifically allege:

### Defiance of Congress

- Failure to produce materials and willfully disobeying a lawful subpoena issued by the House Oversight Committee.

### Abuse of Power

- Allegations that she used the Department of Justice to shield political allies—including Donald Trump—while withholding evidence of sexual abuse and trafficking.

### Dismantling the Rule of Law

- Accusations of firing career public servants for political reasons and ordering investigations into political opponents.

### Perjury

- Allegations that Bondi provided misleading or false testimony during her February appearance before the House Judiciary Committee.

## Bondi Subpoenaed

Simultaneously, House Oversight Chairman James Comer (R-KY) formally served Bondi with a subpoena today for a closed-door deposition scheduled for April 14, 2026.

## Compel Her Testimony

This follows a bipartisan 2026 vote where several Republicans joined Democrats to compel her testimony regarding the roughly 2.5 million files that remains unreleased or heavily redacted.

- The primary lawsuit was filed in the US District Court for the District of Columbia.

### Late August 2025
