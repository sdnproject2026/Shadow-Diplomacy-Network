## Shadow Diplomacy Network

80 years, and still going strong.

The SDN Project is focused on a consensus reality where documentary evidence may be found (now - on-line) pertaining to widely (mis)reported events in which an "official" narrative is not internally consistent (...often contradictory) and/or information "gaps" have been documented.

Focusing on a single subject area and on a single time-frame (1920s - 2020s, mostly 1940s-1980s) constrains the informational boundaries of the SDN Project.

## Associative Memory On Steroids

Individual efforts (associative memory) and on-line tools lend themselves to large-scale pattern recognition, identifying the "gaps" and "inconsistencies" where each investigator makes efforts to factually fill in the gaps (write chapters, one per gap) and/or map the inconsistencies (write chapters, one per inconsistency).

## Sources And Questions

Each chapter ends with a footnote listing "sources" (URL's to webpages) and 5 major questions - prompting further explorations - logically leading to exponential rabbit-holing - to the 5th power.

Requiring responses to queries to list 5 related questions reveals what you do not know that you do not know. Exciting surprises await all investigators.

## Email Distribution

Beyond idle curiosity, the current snapshot of chapters (26) are anonymously pasted to 3-4 public websites (redundancy) and the URL's (26x4) are emailed to ~200 prominent news outlets, officials, influencers, etc. triggering further awareness and research.

## Email Distribution Lists

The email distribution lists are all publicly published as well, encouraging open communication and recognizing the global reach (FOMO) and diversity of interested parties.

## Crowd-sourcing

The intent is to rely on crowd-sourcing with a low bar to entry and transparent anonymous participation.

All individual creators, readers, and editors are encouraged to repaste each version of each chapter and list (no copyright restrictions).

## Additions And Modifications

The published chapters and distribution lists are structured for expansion as new topic areas and newly identified classes of interested parties are identified.

A system has been created to add chapters and distribution lists, to "crowd-source" enhancements (edits) to each chapter and list - publicly and anonymously (GitHub et al, redundancy), and to email URL's pointing to those additions and edits.

> think - investigate - type - paste

> helpful "how-to" pastes are available publicly

## Global In Scope

The chapters, email addresses, and system are all global, are not limited to one country, as are the paste sites (webpages) hosting each copy of each version of each chapter and list - spanning legal jurisdictions and complicating "shutdown" efforts.

## Wikipedia-lite

In all, this system is a homegrown, purpose-built, very distributed version of "Wikipedia" employing existing tools in novel ways, supporting publication, transparent version control, and redundancy - coupled with an email notification system.
