# Emmy Tayler

## EFTA00106062

The document identifier EFTA00106062 is a primary record within the Epstein Files Transparency Act (EFTA) discovery production.

This bates-stamped series was used by federal authorities to map the logistical and financial lifeblood of Jeffrey Epstein’s operation. 

Within these files, an Assistant United States Attorney (AUSA) officially designated the individual associated with this specific record as a "most critical person" (Emmy Tayler), signifying their unique oversight of the enterprise's liquidity.

## Professional History

Emmy Tayler’s career is defined by her transition from a high-level personal assistant to a figure of intense legal interest within the Epstein Files Transparency Act investigation.

### 1997 - 2001

- Employed in London and New York as a personal assistant to Ghislaine Maxwell. Her role involved managing Maxwell’s schedule, travel, and household staff.

### 1999 - 2002

- Frequently appeared on Epstein’s flight logs (e.g., tail number N212JE) during trips to Little St. James and Paris. 

### 2002

- Relocated to Los Angeles, California, to pursue a career in the film and television industry, effectively ceasing her day-to-day employment with Maxwell.

### 2005

- Although early media reports incorrectly placed her at the center of the Palm Beach investigation, court records confirm she was living in Los Angeles during this period.

### 2007

- Returned to the United Kingdom to reside permanently.

### 2021 - 07

- Received a formal public apology and damages from HarperCollins after successfully proving she was defamed in the book _Perversion of Justice_, which had incorrectly placed her at a 2005 crime scene.

### Significance

- Tayler represents the "inner circle" of administrative staff whose testimony is sought to differentiate between legitimate household management and the logistical support of a trafficking ring. 

- Her successful legal defense against defamation has become a landmark case regarding the accuracy of witness testimony in the Epstein Files Transparency Act records.
