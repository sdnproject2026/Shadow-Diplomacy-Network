# Richard Kahn

Richard Kahn, Jeffrey Epstein’s longtime in-house accountant and co-executor of his estate, provided conflicting testimony regarding a woman known as “Jane Doe 4.” 

This individual made serious, albeit unsubstantiated, allegations of sexual abuse against both Epstein and Donald Trump dating back to the 1980s and 1990s. 

Kahn's shifting statements occurred during and after a high-profile deposition before the House Oversight Committee in March 2026, fueling public debate over the estate's transparency and potential "hush money" settlements.

## 1990s - 2019

- Richard Kahn serves as Epstein's primary in-house accountant, managing a complex web of over 60 business entities and shell companies.

- Kahn and attorney Darren Indyke exercise signatory authority over Epstein’s bank accounts, overseeing approximately $1.3 billion in wire transfers flagged by JPMorgan Chase as suspicious.

- Kahn later claims he was unaware of Epstein’s sex trafficking activities during this tenure, despite managing the financial infrastructure that funded the operation.

## 2019-07 - 2019-10

- FBI agents conduct four separate interviews with a woman identifying as "Jane Doe 4" 

- (later linked to the pseudonym "Katie Johnson" in some contexts).

- She alleges that Epstein and Trump sexually abused her in the early 1980s and 1990s, with one interview specifically detailing a building in New York or New Jersey where she was introduced to Trump.

- The FBI records (Form 302s) describe her claims as explicit but note they contain "outlandish" elements and chronological contradictions, leading to no federal charges.

## 2026-03-05

- The U.S. Department of Justice releases previously "missing" FBI interview memos as part of the EFTA document production, bringing the "Jane Doe 4" allegations against Trump into the public spotlight.

## 2026-03-11

- Richard Kahn is deposed behind closed doors by the House Oversight Committee for nearly seven hours.

- In response to questioning by Rep. Ro Khanna, Kahn initially testifies that "Jane Doe 4" received a settlement from the Epstein estate.

- Later in the same deposition, after consulting with his attorney Daniel Ruzumna, Kahn reverses this statement, claiming he does not recognize the name as someone who filed a claim.

## 2026-03-12

- Kahn's attorney contacts committee staff to "correct" the record, stating that "Jane Doe 4" did file a claim, but it was denied and no settlement was reached.

- In a subsequent call later that same day, the attorney shifts again, stating he "can neither confirm nor deny" whether a settlement occurred, citing the fact that multiple women used the pseudonym "Jane Doe 4."

## 2026-03-13

- Rep. Robert Garcia and Rep. Ro Khanna issue a joint statement demanding Kahn "come clean," characterizing his testimony as "contradictory and concerning."

- Public commentary on Reddit and news platforms focuses on whether the estate used a confidential settlement to suppress allegations involving a sitting political figure.
