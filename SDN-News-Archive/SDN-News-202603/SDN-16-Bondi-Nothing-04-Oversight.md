# House Oversight Committee

## 2026-03-18

- Attorney General Pam Bondi and Deputy Attorney General Todd Blanche conducted a closed-door briefing for the House Oversight Committee regarding the ongoing Jeffrey Epstein sex trafficking investigation and the release of millions of related Department of Justice (DOJ) files.

### Walkout By Democratics

- Tensions escalated quickly, leading to a mass walkout by Democratic lawmakers less than an hour into the session.

### Fake Hearing

- Rep. Robert Garcia and other Democrats characterized the briefing as a "fake hearing," citing the lack of an oath, the absence of opening statements, and the three-minute limit on lawmaker questions.

- The walkout was reportedly triggered by a derogatory remark made by Chairman James Comer toward a Democratic member during a heated exchange over the enforcement of a subpoena against Bondi.

### Every Single Perpetrator

- Attorney General Bondi defended the DOJ's work, stating the department is "going after every single perpetrator" and dismissed claims of a cover-up as false narratives.

## 2026-03-17

- House Oversight Committee Chairman James Comer officially subpoenaed Attorney General Pam Bondi to appear for a formal deposition on April 14, 2026.

- The subpoena followed a bipartisan 2025 vote and widespread criticism regarding the DOJ's "haphazard" redactions in the Epstein files, which allegedly exposed victim details while withholding key information.
