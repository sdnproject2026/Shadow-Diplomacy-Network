# Kushner - China - Qatar

The following details outline the real estate transactions involving the Kushner family and international interests from China and Qatar as detailed in the investigative notes and related EFTA disclosures.

## 2007-01

### Acquisition of 666 Fifth Avenue

- Kushner Companies, led by Jared Kushner, purchased the office tower at 666 Fifth Avenue for a record-breaking $1.8 billion.

- The deal was financed almost entirely with debt ($1.75 billion), leaving the property highly vulnerable to the subsequent 2008 financial crisis.

## 2015 - 2016

### Early Negotiations with Qatar

- Jared and Charles Kushner held direct negotiations with Sheikh Hamad bin Jassim al-Thani (HBJ), a billionaire and former Prime Minister of Qatar.

- HBJ agreed in principle to invest up to $500 million, provided the Kushners could secure the remaining necessary capital from other creditors.

- The deal ultimately collapsed when additional funding was not found.

## 2016-10 - 2017-03

### Anbang Insurance Group Negotiations

- Kushner Companies entered into advanced talks with the Chinese-owned Anbang Insurance Group for a $4 billion redevelopment project.

- The proposed "sweetheart deal" would have seen Anbang invest $400 million in equity and arrange a $4 billion construction loan, valuing the tower at an aggressive $2.85 billion.

- Negotiations were terminated in March 2017 following intense public and political scrutiny regarding potential conflicts of interest.

## 2017-04

### Qatari Government

- Charles Kushner met with Qatari Finance Minister Ali Sharif Al Emadi to explicitly request a $1 billion investment from the Qatari sovereign wealth fund (QIA).

- The Qatari government declined the request, citing the poor business logic of the proposal.

- Notably, this refusal occurred just weeks before the US supported a diplomatic blockade against Qatar.

## 2017-05

### China EB-5 "Golden Visa"

- Nicole Kushner Meyer, Jared's sister, headlined investment seminars in Beijing and Shanghai to recruit Chinese investors for the "One Journal Square" project in Jersey City.

- Marketing materials prominently mentioned Jared Kushner’s role in the White House to lure investors with the promise of US Green Cards through the EB-5 program.

## 2018-05 - 2018-08

### Brookfield Asset Management Bailout

- Kushner Companies secured a $1.28 billion deal with Brookfield Asset Management for a 99-year lease on 666 Fifth Avenue, paid upfront.

- While Brookfield claimed no Qatari involvement, it was later revealed that the Qatari Investment Authority (QIA) was the second-largest investor in the specific fund used for the deal, effectively providing the capital that rescued the Kushner property.

# Private Financial Distress

The investigative notes labeled EFTA0104432, released under the Epstein Files Transparency Act in early 2026, provide a detailed look into the intersection of the Kushner family's private financial distress and US foreign policy shifts in 2017.

According to the documents, the connection between the Qatari investment refusal and the diplomatic blockade is supported by the following specific evidence:

## 2017-04

### The Refusal at 666 Fifth Avenue

The notes document a meeting in April 2017 between Charles Kushner and Qatari Finance Minister Ali Sharif Al Emadi.

Kushner formally solicited a direct investment of approximately $1 billion from the Qatar Investment Authority (QIA) to pay down a looming $1.4 billion mortgage on the family’s flagship Manhattan tower.

The Qatari delegation explicitly declined the request, with investigative notes citing Al Emadi’s assessment that the deal lacked "commercial viability" and presented "significant political risk" given Jared Kushner's role in the White House.

## 2017-05

### The Policy Pivot

EFTA0104432 includes internal FBI summaries of "non-traditional diplomatic channels" active throughout May 2017.

The notes detail how Jared Kushner, bypassing State Department protocols led by then-Secretary Rex Tillerson, engaged in private discussions with Saudi and UAE officials regarding a "strategic realignment" in the Gulf.

Informant statements within the file allege that Kushner's support for a hardline stance against Qatar intensified immediately following the formal notification of the QIA's final refusal to invest.

## 2017-06-05

### The Blockade

The Blockade and White House Response

The document tracks the timeline of the Saudi-led blockade, which began on June 5, 2017.

It highlights a discrepancy between the State Department’s call for de-escalation and President Trump’s tweets praising the blockade - a shift the notes attribute to briefings provided by Kushner.

Investigative "sidebars" in the document question whether the "sudden identification of Qatar as the 'funder of terror' was a policy conclusion or a retaliatory measure" for the failed 666 Fifth Avenue bailout.

## 2018-05

### The "Laundered" Resolution

The notes provide a post-script on the resolution of the debt.

In May 2018, Brookfield Asset Management signed a 99-year lease on the building, providing the Kushners with the exact capital needed to satisfy their creditors.

EFTA0104432 notes that the Qatari government (via the QIA) was a major investor in the specific Brookfield fund used for the deal.

The file concludes that while the official blockade remained for years, the "Kushner personal crisis" was resolved using the very Qatari funds that were initially withheld, suggesting a complex quid pro quo involving the cessation of Kushner's personal opposition to Qatari interests.
