# Heritage / Project 2025

## Leadership And Staff  

### Kevin Roberts

- President of The Heritage Foundation

- assumed overall leadership of Project 2025 after Paul Dans stepped down and is described as the key figure driving the initiative.

### Paul Dans

- Founding director of Project 2025, former chief of staff at the U.S. Office of Personnel Management

- co‑editor of the “Mandate for Leadership 2025: The Conservative Promise.”

### Steven Groves

- Co‑editor of the Mandate for Leadership 2025 volume and senior Heritage fellow involved in the project’s policy drafting.

### Roger Severino

- Vice president for domestic policy at Heritage, identified as a principal policy architect for Project 2025’s agenda, especially on social issues.

### Spencer Chretien

- Associate director of Project 2025, former special assistant to President Trump

- frequently named as a top operational leader in the project.

### Derrick Morgan

- Executive vice president at Heritage

- credited in Mandate for Leadership for major work reviewing and shaping the manuscript.

### Wesley Coopersmith

- Chief of staff at Heritage

- acknowledged as part of the internal leadership team assisting the project.

### Therese Pennefather

- Director of Research Editors at Heritage

- led the editorial team that turned dozens of authors’ work into a unified Mandate for Leadership volume.

### William T. Poole

- Senior editor at Heritage

- part of the core editing team for the Mandate.

### Marla Hess

- Editor on the Mandate editorial team.

### Jessica Lowther

- Editor on the Mandate editorial team.

### Karina Rollins

- Editor on the Mandate editorial team.

### Jordan Embree

- Staff member thanked for “immeasurable assistance” to Project 2025 work.

### Sarah Calvis

- Staff member thanked for “immeasurable assistance.”

### Jonathan Moy

- Staff member thanked for “immeasurable assistance.”

## Trump‑world Allies  

### John McEntee

- Former Trump White House Presidential Personnel Office director

- later a senior adviser connected with Heritage on integrating personnel work with Trump‑aligned efforts.

### Ben Carson

- Former Secretary of Housing and Urban Development under Trump

- listed as an author or contributor in the Project 2025 policy agenda. 

### John Ratcliffe

- Former Director of National Intelligence under Trump

- listed as an author or contributor.

### Peter Navarro

- Former White House trade adviser

- listed as an author or contributor to Project 2025 materials. 

## Authors And Contributors

### Daren Bakst

- Deputy Director, Center for Energy and Environment, and Senior Fellow at the Competitive Enterprise Institute

- cited as an author in the Mandate for Leadership and a former Heritage senior fellow.

### Michael Pack

- Former CEO of the Claremont Institute

- listed as author of the chapter on media agencies and the Corporation for Public Broadcasting. 

### Chad Padgett

- Former Bureau of Land Management official in Alaska

- contributor on Interior Department topics. 

### Leah Pedersen

- Contributor associated with USAID‑related content.

### Jacklyn Ward

- Linked to the American Cornerstone Institute

- contributor with HUD press‑relations background. 

### Emma Waters

- Heritage staff member listed as a contributor.
