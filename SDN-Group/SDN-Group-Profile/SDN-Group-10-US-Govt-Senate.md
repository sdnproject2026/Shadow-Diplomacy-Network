# Senate Members

These members either sponsored the Senate version of the **Epstein Files Transparency Act** or have recently accused the DOJ of a "cover-up" regarding unreleased pages.

## Democratic Senators

### Sen. Jeff Merkley (D-OR)

- Primary sponsor of the Senate Epstein Files Transparency Act.

- https://www.merkley.senate.gov/contact

### Sen. Ben Ray Luján (D-NM)

- Lead co-sponsor; vocal critic of DOJ delays in releasing the full 5-million-page trove.

- https://www.lujan.senate.gov/contact

### Sen. Chuck Schumer (D-NY)

- Senate Minority Leader; pushed for the elimination of statutes of limitations for victims and led the demand for immediate passage.

- https://www.schumer.senate.gov/contactemail-chuck

### Sen. Richard Durbin (D-IL)

- Judiciary Committee lead; involved in oversight regarding the "Rule of Five" to compel document production.

- https://www.durbin.senate.gov/contactemail

### Sen. Martin Heinrich (D-NM)

- Vocal advocate for victims' justice; recently demanded the release of all "hidden" names mentioned in files.

- https://www.heinrich.senate.gov/contact

## Republican Senators

### Sen. Rand Paul (R-KY)

- One of the first GOP Senators to break ranks and co-sponsor the Transparency Act in late 2025.

- https://www.paul.senate.gov/connect/email-rand/

### Sen. Lisa Murkowski (R-AK)

- Bipartisan co-sponsor of the Act; emphasized the need for "equal justice" regardless of the subjects' status.

- https://www.murkowski.senate.gov/contactemail

### Sen. Chuck Grassley (R-IA)

- Long-time advocate for judicial transparency; sponsored the Sunshine in the Courtroom Act related to broadcasting these high-profile proceedings.

- https://www.grassley.senate.gov/contactquestions-and-comments

### Sen. John Cornyn (R-TX)

- Member of the Judiciary Committee; vocal regarding the oversight of the DOJ's handling of the 2008 non-prosecution agreement.

- https://www.cornyn.senate.gov/contact

### Sen. Marsha Blackburn (R-TN)

- Heavily involved in the Judiciary Committee’s investigation into Epstein’s flight logs and the procurement of the "Black Book."

- https://www.blackburn.senate.gov/email-me
