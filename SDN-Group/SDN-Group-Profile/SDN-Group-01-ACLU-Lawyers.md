# ACLU lawyers

USA ACLU lawyers have primarily focused on labor trafficking and victims' rights cases, with limited direct involvement in sex trafficking, RICO statutes, or government coverups.

## 2005

Jennifer Arnett

- ACLU Women's Rights Project  

Led federal lawsuit against NY hotel under Victims of Trafficking and Violence Protection Act for sexual harassment and forced labor of Latina workers.

## 2009

Lenora Lapidus

- Director, ACLU Women's Rights Project  

Directed ACLU Massachusetts v. Sebelius challenging federal funding cuts affecting trafficking victims' reproductive health access.

## 2015

ACLU cooperating counsel

Assisted RICO and Trafficking Victims Protection Act claims in David v. Signal International federal case on post-Katrina guestworker exploitation.
