# House Members

Below are 10 currently serving and active House members (5 Democrats, 5 Republicans) who are leading the push for unredacted records and have not announced an exit from their terms.

## Democratic Representatives

These members are currently using their committee positions to subpoena the DOJ for the 3 million pages still allegedly being withheld.

### Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA)

- Role: The primary author of the Epstein Files Transparency Act. He recently used his floor privilege to name redacted individuals found in the January 2026 data dump.

- https://khanna.house.gov/contact

### Rep. Robert Garcia (D-CA)

- Role: Ranking Member of the Oversight Committee. He has spearheaded the "Subpoena the Flight Logs" movement and is the lead voice demanding AG Pam Bondi's compliance.

- https://robertgarcia.house.gov/contact

### Rep. Jamie Raskin (D-MD)

- Role: Ranking Member of the Judiciary Committee. He is currently investigating the "selective redaction" process and whether it violates the 2025 Transparency Act.

- https://raskin.house.gov/contact

### Rep. Summer Lee (D-PA)

- Role: Vocal Oversight Committee member who recently moved to hold the DOJ in contempt for the unauthorized redaction of potential co-conspirators.

- https://summerlee.house.gov/contact

### Rep. Darren Soto (D-FL)

- Role: An original co-sponsor who has been vocal about the failures of the early Florida-based investigations mentioned in the new files.

- https://soto.house.gov/contact

## Republican Representatives

These members have focused on "exposing the deep state" and ensuring that names of high-ranking officials in the files are not shielded by federal agencies.

### Rep. Thomas Massie (R-KY)

- Role: The lead Republican co-author of the Act. He famously joined Ro Khanna in a private DOJ viewing room and is currently pushing to appoint a "Special Master" to oversee all future releases.

- https://massie.house.gov/contact

### Rep. Tim Burchett (R-TN)

- Role: A long-time transparency advocate who has been vocal on social media and in hearings about "naming every single person" on the flight manifests.

- https://burchett.house.gov/contact

### Rep. Mike Flood (R-NE)

- Role: A key supporter of the bill's passage; he has focused on the legal requirements for the DOJ to release grand jury records that were previously sealed.

- https://flood.house.gov/contact

### Rep. Wesley Hunt (R-TX)

- Role: A vocal member of the Judiciary Committee who has questioned the DOJ’s "slow-walking" of the remaining 3 million documents.

- https://hunt.house.gov/contact

### Rep. Anna Paulina Luna (R-FL)

- Role: Known for her aggressive stance on government transparency, she has consistently demanded that the FBI release all "Black Book" contacts without redaction.

- https://luna.house.gov/contact
