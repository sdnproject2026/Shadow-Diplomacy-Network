# Foreign Relations

Living elected and appointed US federal officials from the 1970s-1990s whom served on intelligence or foreign relations committees or roles.

## 1965-1999

### Lee Hamilton

- D-IN, Elected Representative

House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence member 1970s onward

- House Foreign Affairs subcommittee chair.

## 1981-1995

### Dave McCurdy

- D-OK, Elected Representative

House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence member and chair 1991-1993.

## 1989-2004

### Porter Goss

- R-FL, Elected Representative

House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence member late 1980s, chair 1997-2004.

## 1987-2023

### Richard Shelby

- R-AL, Elected Senator

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence member late 1980s, chair 1997-2001.

## 1975-1993

### Carla Anderson Hills

- Appointed

HUD Secretary 1975-1977

- US Trade Representative 1989-1993 handling foreign relations.

## 1981-1993

### Vin Weber

- R-MN, Elected Representative

House member focused on foreign policy

- later appointed Aspen Institute trustee on foreign affairs.

## 1975-1993

### Timothy E. Wirth

- D-CO, Elected

House 1975-1987 on Foreign Affairs

- Senate 1987-1993

- later appointed UN role in foreign relations.
