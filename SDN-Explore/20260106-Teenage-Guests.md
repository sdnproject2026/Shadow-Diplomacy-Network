
# Teenage Guests

Recent investigative reporting by *The New York Times* has detailed the historical relationship between Donald Trump and Jeffrey Epstein, focusing on their shared social circles and interactions involving young women during the 1990s.

## Late 1994

Tina Davis, a model then with Ford Models, was instructed by her booker to attend a party at Mar-a-Lago where she observed Jeffrey Epstein and Donald Trump socializing together.

## December 20, 2025

The New York Times* published a major investigation titled "'Don's Best Friend': How Epstein and Trump Bonded Over the Pursuit of Women," citing more than 30 sources including former employees and witnesses.

## December 23, 2024 to date

A series of document releases from the Department of Justice and grand jury records in New York and Florida have provided fresh context for the relationship, including photos of the two men and records of frequent visits.

## January 3, 2026

During recent public discourse following these releases, former associates and survivors have come forward to describe Epstein and Trump as "very close" during the mid-1990s, contradicting later attempts by Trump to distance himself.

## January 6, 2026

New reports highlight that Epstein allegedly introduced Trump to at least six women who later made allegations of grooming or abuse, a claim that the former president has consistently denied.
