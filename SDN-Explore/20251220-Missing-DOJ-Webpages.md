# DOJ Webpages Disappeared

At least 16 files related to Jeffrey Epstein have disappeared from the Department of Justice (DOJ) public webpage, including a photograph of President Donald Trump, less than a day after they were made available on Friday, December 19, 2025. The removal occurred by Saturday, December 20, 2025, with no explanation from the DOJ and no public notice.

### December 19, 2025

At least 15–16 files were released by the DOJ on Friday, December 19, 2025, as part of a large document dump related to Jeffrey Epstein investigations.
- [1][2][4]

The files included images of paintings depicting nude women, a series of photographs along a credenza and in drawers, and one image showing President Trump, Melania Trump, Epstein, and Ghislaine Maxwell.
- [6][8]

### December 20, 2025

By Saturday, December 20, 2025, these files were no longer accessible on the DOJ’s public website.
- [2][3][8][1]

The missing files also included heavily redacted documents, such as a 119-page grand jury transcript marked “Grand Jury-NY,” which was entirely blacked out.
- [4]

The DOJ did not issue any statement regarding the disappearance of these files or the reason for their removal.
- [2][4][6]
