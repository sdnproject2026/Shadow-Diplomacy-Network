# 18,000 Epstein emails

Bloomberg News obtained over 18,000 emails from Jeffrey Epstein's private Yahoo account in September 2025, revealing previously unknown details about Epstein’s connections and activities, especially regarding Ghislaine Maxwell and a wide web of powerful associates.

### Event Timeline and Key Dates

September 10–11, 2025*

- News breaks that Bloomberg News has acquired 18,000 emails from Epstein’s personal Yahoo account. This trove was cryptographically verified and subjected to metadata analysis before release.

September 11, 2025*

- Multiple Bloomberg investigations and podcasts highlight the cache, focusing on the deep ties between Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell, including secret financial arrangements, fertility procedures, and efforts to discredit accusers.

September 25–26, 2025*

- Further reporting focuses on how these emails reveal Epstein’s elite support network as he faced charges, showing communications with lawyers, academics, and crisis management experts.

### Details revealed by the emails

- The emails span a 20-year period but are most concentrated between 2005 and 2008, coinciding with Epstein’s criminal investigation and prosecution for sex offenses in Florida.

- Maxwell exercised significant influence, opening bank accounts and managing assets for Epstein, contradicting her public claims of distancing after the early 2000s.

- They strategized about legal risks, attempted to discredit victims, and arranged gifts for associates and victims, with a spreadsheet itemizing nearly $1.8 million in luxury payments.

- The emails mention several prominent figures, including a move by Epstein to remove Donald Trump from a “power list” in September 2006, and describe Maxwell’s role in shaping Epstein’s social network.

### Impact and Verification

- Bloomberg used cryptographic and metadata analysis to vet and authenticate the collection, cross-referencing with public records.

- The revelations generated significant legal, political, and media responses, with the White House and other involved parties swiftly denying controversial associations or roles implied by the emails.

The event marks a major new chapter in the investigation and public understanding of Epstein’s network and operations, largely driven by the journalistic efforts of Bloomberg and its reporters throughout September 2025.
