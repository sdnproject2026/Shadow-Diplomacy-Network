# My Friends Will Get Hurt

Today’s news regarding the falling out between President Donald Trump and Representative Marjorie Taylor Greene centers on explosive allegations regarding the release of Jeffrey Epstein’s case files. Reports indicate a significant verbal altercation took place over the phone, highlighting a deep rift in the Republican party over transparency and the identities of Epstein’s associates.

## December 29, 2025

- Reported Confrontation over Epstein Files

Reports surfaced detailing a phone call where President Trump allegedly screamed at Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene so loudly that staffers in her office could hear the exchange. The confrontation reportedly occurred after Greene pressured the President to allow the full disclosure of Jeffrey Epstein’s accomplices.

- The "My Friends Will Get Hurt" Allegation

During the alleged call, Greene reportedly questioned the President's opposition to exposing those involved with Epstein. Trump is said to have snapped back that the release would not happen because "my friends will get hurt," a statement that has sparked intense political scrutiny.

## December 23, 2025

- DOJ Releases Initial Epstein Documents

The Department of Justice released nearly 30,000 pages of additional documents related to the Epstein investigation. While the documents mention Trump’s name in the context of past flights and social connections, the DOJ issued a statement noting that some materials include "untrue and sensationalist claims" submitted to the FBI years prior.

## December 8, 2025

- Greene’s "60 Minutes" Interview Airs

In a televised interview, Rep. Greene detailed her fallout with Trump, confirming he was "furious" with her for signing a discharge petition to force the release of the Epstein files. She claimed Trump specifically warned her that the disclosure would "hurt people."

- Trump Labels Greene a "Traitor"

Responding to the interview on Truth Social, Trump referred to the Georgia representative as "Marjorie 'Traitor' Brown" (a play on her maiden name). He dismissed her claims, suggesting she had been "jilted" and was no longer part of the MAGA movement.

## November 21, 2025

- Greene Announces Resignation

Following weeks of public attacks from the President and his supporters, Rep. Greene announced her intention to resign from Congress, effective January 5, 2026. She cited a "toxic" political culture and threats against her family as primary reasons for her departure.
