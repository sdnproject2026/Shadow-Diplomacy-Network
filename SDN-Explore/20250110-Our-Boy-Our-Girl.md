# 2025 Russian State TV Broadcast Claims

## 2025 - 01

### Russian State TV Remarks About Kash Patel And Tulsi Gabbard

* During a January 2025 broadcast on the Russia-1 television program 'Evening With Vladimir Solovyov', host Vladimir Solovyov referred to Kash Patel as "our boy" and Tulsi Gabbard as "our girl".

* The discussion reportedly centered on anticipated leadership appointments involving the FBI and U.S. intelligence agencies.

* Solovyov reportedly stated, "When will they appoint our boy to the FBI?" while discussing Patel.

* He also reportedly asked, "And what about our girl? We have our girl there" while discussing Gabbard.

* The comments were broadcast on the Russian state-controlled television channel Russia-1.

* Multiple English-language outlets and monitoring groups circulated translated clips and transcripts of the segment.

## Key Details

### Broadcast Program

* Program title: 'Evening With Vladimir Solovyov'

* Network: Russia-1

* Host: Vladimir Solovyov

* Format: Political talk show featuring Kremlin-aligned commentary and panel discussions.

### Commentary About Kash Patel

* Kash Patel was discussed in relation to possible FBI leadership.

### Commentary About Tulsi Gabbard

* Tulsi Gabbard was discussed in relation to U.S. intelligence leadership.

## Broader Context

### Russian State Media Framing

* Analysts and monitoring organizations have noted that Russian state media personalities sometimes refer to sympathetic or strategically useful Western political figures using possessive language such as "our".

* These references are generally interpreted as propaganda framing rather than evidence of formal affiliation.
