## Documented Historical Allegations

The text you provided contains a mix of **documented historical allegations** and **newly unsealed but unverified files** from 2025. 

While the specific quote exists within official government records, the "breaking" framing is highly misleading.

### April 2016

A woman using the pseudonym "Katie Johnson" filed a federal lawsuit in California against Donald Trump and Jeffrey Epstein, alleging she was raped at age 13 in 1994. The lawsuit was initially dismissed due to technical filing errors.

### October 2016

The plaintiff refiled the lawsuit in New York. The text you provided, including the quote regarding the "fancy hotel or building," appeared in the legal affidavits and declarations supporting this specific civil case.

### November 4, 2016

The plaintiff voluntarily withdrew the lawsuit just days before the presidential election. Her attorney, Lisa Bloom, cited "numerous threats" the woman had received as the reason for dropping the case. No court ever ruled on the truth of the allegations.

### October 2020

Internal FBI document **EFTA00020518** was created. This record, which has recently become public, contains a summary of the 2016 "Katie Johnson" claims. It includes the "stone cold" demeanor description and the specific rape accusation you cited.

### April 25, 2025

Virginia Giuffre, the most prominent Epstein accuser, died by suicide in Australia at the age of 41. While she was a major witness, she was not the "Katie Johnson" plaintiff mentioned in your text. There is no evidence that the "Katie Johnson" accuser is dead.

### July 7, 2025

The Department of Justice and the FBI issued a formal memo regarding their review of Epstein-related files. They stated they found no credible evidence that Epstein blackmailed prominent individuals and found no evidence to predicate new investigations against uncharged third parties.

### November 19, 2025

President Trump signed the **Epstein Files Transparency Act**, which legally mandated the public release of all unclassified FBI and DOJ records related to Jeffrey Epstein within 30 days.

### December 23, 2025

The DOJ released a massive 30,000-page tranche of documents. 

The specific FBI document you quoted was part of this release. 

However, the DOJ and major news outlets clarified that these files often contain unverified tips and old allegations that the FBI recorded but never proved or acted upon.
