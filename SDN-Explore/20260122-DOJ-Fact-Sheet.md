# Department of Justice Website

The official Department of Justice (DOJ) website for materials released under the Epstein Files Transparency Act is the Epstein Library.


## Main Landing Page

 - https://www.justice.gov/epstein

## Searchable Database

-  https://www.justice.gov/epstein/search

## Specific DOJ Disclosures

 - https://www.justice.gov/epstein/doj-disclosures 

## Four Primary Categories

### Court Records

- Documents from criminal and civil cases involving Jeffrey Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell.

### DOJ Disclosures

- Materials released specifically to comply with the Epstein Files Transparency Act (H.R. 4405).

### FOIA Records

- Documents previously released through Freedom of Information Act requests.

### House Committee Disclosures

- Records provided to the House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform.
