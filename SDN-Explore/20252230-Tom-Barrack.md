# Thomas J. Barrack, Jr.

Thomas J. Barrack Jr. is an American private equity investor and diplomat currently serving as the United States Ambassador to Turkey. 

A long-time friend and ally of Donald Trump, Barrack has spent four decades navigating the intersection of global finance and international politics.

## Life

### April 28, 1947

Born in Culver City, California, to Lebanese Christian parents.

### 1969

Earned a Bachelor of Arts degree from the University of Southern California (USC), where he played on the varsity rugby team.

### 1972

Received a Juris Doctor from the University of San Diego School of Law; he also attended the USC Gould School of Law where he served as an editor for the Southern California Law Review.

## Work History

### 1970s

Began his career as a finance attorney at the law firm of Herbert W. Kalmbach, who served as President Richard Nixon’s personal lawyer.

### Late 1970s

Worked in Saudi Arabia as a project finance lawyer for firms including Fluor, Bechtel, and Aramco.

### 1980s

Served as President and CEO of Dunn International Corporation, a real estate development firm.

### 1982

Appointed by President Ronald Reagan as Deputy Undersecretary of the U.S. Department of the Interior.

### Mid-1980s

Worked on Wall Street as a Senior Vice President at E.F. Hutton & Co.

### Late 1980s

Served as a Principal with the Robert M. Bass Group, an investment vehicle for billionaire Robert Bass.

### 1991

Founded Colony Capital, a global private equity firm (now known as DigitalBridge) specializing in distressed real estate assets.

### 2017

Served as Chairman of the 58th Presidential Inaugural Committee for Donald Trump.

### 2025

Confirmed as the U.S. Ambassador to the Republic of Türkiye and appointed as Special Envoy for Syria.

## Family

### Parents and Heritage

He is the son of Lebanese immigrants; his father managed a local grocery store.

### Children

Public records indicate he has children from previous marriages, though specific names are often kept private in official diplomatic biographies.

## Board Memberships

Has served on the boards of Carrefour S.A. (French retailer), First Republic Bank, Accor S.A. (hotel group), and Continental Airlines.

## Philanthropy and Education

A Trustee at the University of Southern California and recipient of the Golden Plate Award from the American Academy of Achievement.

## International Honors

Awarded the Chevalier de la Légion d’Honneur by French President Nicolas Sarkozy in 2010.

## Indictments

### July 20, 2021

Indicted by the Department of Justice on seven counts, including acting as an unregistered agent of the United Arab Emirates (UAE), conspiracy, obstruction of justice, and making false statements to the FBI.

### November 4, 2022

Acquitted by a federal jury in New York on all nine counts after a high-profile trial where his defense argued his actions were part of legitimate business and personal networking.

## Politics

### Republican Party

A long-standing member and major fundraiser for Republican candidates.

### Relationship with Donald Trump

A close personal friend of Trump for over 40 years, serving as a senior advisor to his 2016 campaign and a key bridge to Middle Eastern leaders.

### Diplomatic Stance

Advocates for "transactional diplomacy" and strong centralized states in the Middle East, often emphasizing personal relationships between leaders like Trump and Erdogan.

# 1991 to 2017 Gap

Thomas J. Barrack Jr. established himself as a dominant figure in global private equity between 1991 and 2017. 

During this period, he founded one of the world's largest real estate investment firms and leveraged his Lebanese heritage and language skills to become a key intermediary between Western capital and Middle Eastern sovereign wealth.

## Work History

### 1991

Founded Colony Capital, a private equity firm focused on distressed real estate assets, following his tenure as a principal with the Robert M. Bass Group.

### Early 1990s

Achieved 50% profits in the firm's first two years by purchasing bad real estate loans from "busted" Savings and Loans (S&Ls) and the federal Resolution Trust Corporation.

### 2005

Named "Entrepreneur of the Year" by the USC Lloyd Greif Center for Entrepreneurial Studies.

### 2008 – 2010

Purchased Michael Jackson’s Neverland Ranch to prevent foreclosure and acquired $70 million of Jared Kushner’s debt on 666 Fifth Avenue.

### 2010

Partnered with the Qatar Investment Authority to purchase the film production company Miramax for $660 million.

### 2012

Founded Colony American Homes, which became one of the largest owners of single-family rental homes in the U.S. following the Great Recession.

### 2016

Sold Miramax to the Qatari beIN Media Group, reportedly at a fourfold profit.

### 2017

Completed a massive three-way merger between Colony Capital, NorthStar Asset Management, and NorthStar Realty Finance to create Colony NorthStar, a REIT with $58 billion in assets.

## News Items

### October 2005

Featured in a prominent Fortune magazine cover story titled "I'm Tom Barrack and I'm Getting Out," where he accurately predicted the impending real estate market crash.

### June 2012

Elected to the Board of Trustees of the University of Southern California (USC).

### June 2015

The Barrack family committed $15 million to the USC Marshall School of Business to renovate a key campus building (renamed Barrack Hall).

### May 2017

The Guardian reported that Barrack was under investigation in Italy for alleged tax evasion related to a €170 million scheme involving the sale of the Costa Smeralda resort in Sardinia.

## Published Works

### October 2016

Authored an op-ed titled "Why I'm Supporting Donald Trump" (and various policy-related pieces), emphasizing Trump's business acumen and a needed shift in Middle East policy.

### April 2017

Published an article in The Washington Post titled "Our Shared Humanity Is Our Greatest Strength," where he commented on political division and Trump's rhetoric.

## Criminal and Legal

### 1980s (Retrospective)

Disclosed in later interviews that he became disillusioned with government service after being required to testify regarding a gift he paid to the purchaser of Attorney General Edwin Meese's house.

### 2012 – 2017

Colony American Homes faced public criticism and media reports regarding poor tenant treatment, rapid evictions, and lack of property maintenance.

### 2016 – 2017

Activities during this period (such as inserting language into campaign speeches) later became the basis for his 2021 federal indictment for acting as an unregistered foreign agent for the UAE.

## Organizations and Associations

### 2000

Recipient of the Golden Plate Award from the American Academy of Achievement.

### 2010

Knighted as a Chevalier de la Légion d’Honneur by French President Nicolas Sarkozy.

## Board Memberships (1991–2017)

Served on the boards of Carrefour, First Republic Bank, Accor S.A., Continental Airlines, and the American University of Beirut.

# 2017 to 2025 Gap

Thomas J. Barrack Jr. has had a significant career trajectory from 2017 to 2025, transitioning from a private equity executive and informal advisor to a criminal defendant, and finally into a high-level diplomatic role.

## Work History & News Items

### January 20, 2017

- Served as the Chairman of the 58th Presidential Inaugural Committee, which raised a record $107 million for the inauguration of Donald Trump.

### 2017 – 2021

- Acted as an informal advisor to senior U.S. government officials on Middle Eastern foreign policy while continuing to lead Colony Capital (rebranded as DigitalBridge in 2021).

### July 20, 2021

- Federal prosecutors in Brooklyn indicted Barrack on charges of acting as an unregistered agent of the United Arab Emirates (UAE), conspiracy, and making false statements to the FBI.

### November 4, 2022

- A federal jury in New York acquitted Barrack on all nine counts after a seven-week trial.

### December 2024

- Nominated by President-elect Donald Trump to serve as the U.S. Ambassador to Türkiye.

### May 14, 2025

- Officially assumed office as the United States Ambassador to Türkiye following Senate confirmation.

### May 23, 2025

- Appointed as the U.S. Special Envoy for Syria, assuming dual responsibilities in regional diplomacy.

### June – December 2025

- Acted as a central figure in Middle East ceasefire negotiations; proposed a "phased transfer" plan for Hezbollah’s weapons in Lebanon and managed relations with the new interim government in Syria.

## Published Works & Public Statements

### October 2016 – early 2017

- Authored various op-eds and policy memos regarding U.S. energy policy and Middle East relations, some of which were later scrutinized during his 2022 trial.

### March 2023

- Subject of a deep-dive podcast series by WNYC/ProPublica titled "Trump, Inc.", which detailed his "20 Rules for Success" and his business dealings in the Gulf.

### November 2025

- Delivered a keynote speech at the Manama Dialogue in Bahrain, outlining a "Trump Doctrine" for the Middle East that emphasizes sovereignty and "bold action over endless diplomacy."

### December 2025

- Published a high-profile interview with Iran International stating that the U.S. is not pursuing "regime change" in Tehran but seeks a "genuine dialogue" on nuclear issues.
